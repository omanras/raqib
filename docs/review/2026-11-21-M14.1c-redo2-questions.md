# M14.1c REDO 2 - questions, choices and the tests changed (ADR-0007 Amendment CA)

What the builder did for S1 to S7, each choice where the HANDOFF was silent,
and each existing test changed, with why. Nothing here is answered locally.

## The items

- S1 (CA-1). discover's search for act-roots: a new `_sampled(p)` (a record
  that is not the reader's) is asked of the chain's shell as of its root; a
  shell no sample held makes no chain. Tests: the tool sampled, its `sh` and
  git given by the reader alone: no act-root, no tree; the tool and its sh
  sampled, git from the reader: act-root 2020 with sh and git among its acts.
  The byte-for-byte test and every act-root test pass unchanged.
- S2 (CA-2). A HostChild line of a round whose inventory carries
  `from_reader` (discover writes it only where a reader's text was read)
  carries `input_meta.command_sources`, in the order of `command_programs`:
  `sample`, `events` or `both`, from where each program's children were seen
  (`child_seen_by`: the sources their records cite; the process events'
  `procevents`, any other source the sample). A line of any other round has
  no such key. No rule's match reads it (the rule catalog is untouched in its
  matches). Tests: a round with bash (sample and events) and curl (events)
  writes `["both", "events"]`, the next round with no reader's text writes
  `make` with no key; the same rounds with a helper that gives nothing and
  with one that never started write the same lines.
- S3 (CA-3). X36 reads the wget's own entry of `command_sources`: `events`
  MET; `both` n/a with "it lived to a sample: the reader and the sample both
  held it"; `sample` MISSED; no line saying it, a list of another length or
  word, or no sources: MISSED ("whose word it is cannot be told"). A line
  naming curl (`both`) and wget (`events`) is MET. The second review's F8
  (found[0], a None source) is gone with the old reading of the cites.
- S4 (CA-4). The live reader keeps a start taken from the reader's record in
  `child_started.events` = {"from": "the process events: when its fork was
  heard (the reader's record; no /proc read)", "by": {pid: began}}, only
  where there is one; `child_started.by` holds /proc reads alone. `_act_time`
  gives such an act the basis `events`. Test: one round with an ended reader
  child (`events`) and an alive one read from /proc (`stat`).
- S5 (CA-5). BETWEEN_HEARD drops "for the whole watch" and says "(not the
  name it was called by)"; THREAT_MODEL's and PROC-EVENTS' lists of the
  result's holes name the answer that holds back a rest; THREAT_MODEL says
  "write a file of its choosing"; ruleform, NET-TOOL.yaml and DESTRUCT-CMD.yaml
  add ", or under an act-root the samples made" beside "a tree that talks at
  a sample". PROC-EVENTS also says the chain's shell (CA-1), the events start
  (CA-4) and `command_sources` (CA-2). A test holds the sentences.
- S6 (CA-6).
  - A copy of the watch's record written again for the same pid with another
    time or image takes the new time, image and round (and goes to the end of
    the order).
  - A copy forgotten at PE_OWN_MAX is counted (`own["forgot"]`, given to the
    round as `inv["procevents"]["own_forgot"]` only where not 0) and said in
    the section: "copies of the watch's record forgotten at their bound (N),
    so a process of its own told later may be said as a stranger: n".
  - Tests: an ask written in round 2, answered late and empty in round 3,
    then round 4's answer carries the socket tool run after round 2's build:
    no "did not start" hole and `own` 1; the report holds PE_OWN % 1; a copy
    written again takes the new time; a copy forgotten at a bound of 2 is
    said. An autouse fixture of tests/test_procevents_round.py puts the
    process's own record back after each test.
  - Shown failing: with `self.covered = self.rounds` (the round that read the
    answer) in ProcEvents.round, the late-then-empty test and the report test
    failed (`own` 0, no PE_OWN line); with the old rule of `remember` (a pid
    copied once only), the written-again test failed (`own` 0). Both were put
    back and pass.
- S7. The gate (below).

## Choices where the HANDOFF was silent

- C1 (S2). A child's source is read from its cites as the inventory gives
  them (the act's first three records, the line's child keeps two). With
  one snapshot a round, as the watch takes it, a merged child's records are
  the sample's one and the reader's one, so both are cited. With `--window`
  (several snapshots in a round) a child held by three snapshots and the
  reader cites the snapshots only and is said `sample`. Q1: should discover
  give the act its sources (outside this task's discover Scope)?
- C2 (S2). Where one program name stands for several children of a line,
  its entry is the union of theirs (one `sample` and one `events` child of
  the same name: `both`).
- C3 (S2). `_host_lines` keeps its signature (two existing tests wrap it by
  position); the round sets `self.reader_round` before calling it.
- C4 (S6). The forgotten count is a key of the round's `procevents` only
  where it is not 0, so every other round's dict is what it was. A pid
  forgotten at the bound and recorded again is copied again, and counted
  again if forgotten again.
- C5 (S4). Only a reader-only child the reader said ended has its start in
  `events`; a reader child alive at the ask is read from /proc, as before
  (BZ-8's rule).
- C6 (S5). GUIDE's paragraph is unchanged: none of CA-5's sentences is in it
  (its words were changed in the first REDO and tests/test_readme_top.py
  holds them word for word).

  `command_sources`; the X36 tests read it (CA-3).

No other existing test was changed; no rule's fixture or finding changed; no
