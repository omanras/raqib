## 4. G-6 (c): what the pass looks at between beats, and what then goes unsaid
What is looked at between beats, ledger by ledger (eventlog.py:210-237). The "mark" is the first entry not yet said or
  built.

| Ledger | Looked at between beats |
|---|---|
| Trees | Every tree, every pass: built each time, its core compared |
| Findings | Only entries from the mark on; the open set is never filled because no `changes` is given |
| Source findings | Entries from the mark on; earlier entries whose last look had `settled` false or `pending` true;
  and every place in this pass's `same` and the previous pass's (`self.folded`) |
| The watch's holes | Entries from the mark on |
| The notifier's holes | All, every pass |
| Blind spots | New ones only; ones already said are held without a build, even at a beat |
| Notes | Entries from the mark on |
| The lost-words note | Held without a build once said |
| Knock rows | Entries from the mark on, plus those whose last look had status or `called` "folded". A row whose
  `called` was "folded" stays open for good (cost only) |

The mark stops at an entry whose write failed, so everything after it is looked at again on every pass. **[MORE SAID,
  cost]** With an unwritable file (disk full) every pass tries every entry, and "not written (attempts)" grows by the
  size of all the ledgers each round. Medium likelihood under a disk fault.

Changes that host.py makes after an entry was first said, and that the between-beats pass does not look at. Each is
  said only at the next beat: up to one `--beat`, 60 s by default (6 s in the Table's watch), or at the finish if no
  beat falls first. If the watch is killed before that beat, it is never said.
- **Findings.** `notice` is rewritten when a fold is decided (token `(entry,"notice")`, host.py:2389 and 1832). The
  knock row is still open and says it at once, so only the finding's own `knocked.status` is late. **[SILENCE, late;
  low]**
- **Source findings.** "Can still change" is decided by `settled` being false, and an entry that is already settled
  still changes:
    - (i) `_refresh` rewrites `notice`, `judged_on`, `benign_note` and `settled` on act copies whenever the act's
    status, its standing or its note-ness moves (host.py:3985-3991). That happens when a fold of the act's knock is
    decided, when a nearer copy arrives, or when a tree is made benign.
    - When a nearer copy arrives, the new copy is said at once with alarm true, while the old standing copy, already
    settled, stays alarm true until the beat: **two alarms for one act, for up to a beat. [MORE SAID; medium]** (an
    act read by nested trees).
    - When the standing moves to a copy that was said "judged on another tree", the act's one alarm sits on the wrong
    tree until the beat. **[SILENCE; low-medium]**
  - (ii) The notice of a non-act entry, when its fold is decided (host.py:3939). **[SILENCE, late; low]**
    - (iii) `grown`: the summary, cited_seq and lines are rewritten (host.py:3811-3813), so the new cites are late.
    **[SILENCE, late; low]**
    - (iv) The folded half of a pair (G-1), found after both were said, is looked at through `same` and is said at
    once. Its un-folding is looked at through `self.folded`, also at once. Both are correct.
- **The watch's holes.** `notice` by fold (host.py:1802 and 3245). **[SILENCE, late; low]**
- **The notifier's holes.** Nothing is missed.
- **Blind spots.** Their rounds (host.py:2679) are said only at the finish, as ruled.
- **Notes replaced in place.** There are **three** places now: host.py:3736 (pre-M7.8), 3754 (`pre_dest`, from the
  merge) and 3869 (AM-3's count). **[SILENCE, late; low]**
- **Knock rows.** A row whose fold is decided is said at once. But `for` is late when its thing is first written after
  the knock's rev 1 (for example, the thing's write failed once). **[SILENCE, late; low]**
- **Trees.**
  - A NEW-AGENT knock is said at once.
    - A HOST-RHYTHM or HOST-UNWATCHED knock writes `tree["rhythm"]` or `tree["unwatched"]` (host.py:2393-2397).
    `event.tree` reads only `row["knocked"]` (event.py:312), so these are **never** said on the tree event; the
    finding says them. **[SILENCE on the tree; low impact]**
    - `tree["knocked"]` keeps the status at the call ("folded") forever. This predates the REDO, and the report does
    the same (host.py:4389).

So: "settled is False" is not true of every entry that will change. Settled source findings, findings, holes and notes
  all change after they are marked done.

## 5. G-1 in detail
- **Which half is folded.** `told_twice` returns {id(os entry): host entry} (host.py:721-758). The pass sets
  `same[place of the os entry] = about of the host entry` (host.py:1909-1912). The listing leaves out `id(f) in
  pairs`, which is the os entry (host.py:4434, 4440 and 4451). It is the same half.
- **Both halves alarm false?** No. Pairs require equal standing, and `used` keeps a host entry from being taken twice,
  so the kept half is never itself folded.
- **`same_as` pointing at a thing never said?** Yes. If the host half is not built (C-10) or its write fails, the os
  half says `same_as` the host half and the file holds **no** alarm for the act. Only an `event-not-built` hole
  remains. **[SILENCE; low]**
- **Exactly one alarm in every order?** No.
  - Host first, then os: one alarm.
    - Os first: the os half is said at rev 1 with alarm true (ALARM_STATES includes "not settled"), and is folded only
    at rev 2 when the host half arrives.
  - Pair found one round later: the same, two alarm-true lines, the second superseded.
    - A collector that alerts on each alarm line alerts twice. G-1 rules "the folded one's next rev", so this is as
    ruled. **[MORE SAID; medium]** (the os follow runs in its own thread)
- **X18 now compares:**
  - findings by rule id only (a multiset);
  - non-folded source findings by (rule, pid);
  - each by its last rev within the report's `run:`;
  - against the listings of SOURCE FINDINGS and ON BENIGN TREES, with their counts.
- **X18 can be MET with a listed finding missing.** Both halves of a pair have the same rule and pid (same tree). So
  if the host half's event is missing and the os half's last rev is unfolded (told_twice raised during the pass, or
  the folding rev failed to write), the result is MET. Low.
- **X18 can be MISSED with every finding present.** The report prints "pid None", which `thing_of` (check.py:121) does
  not accept. Very low.

## 6. G-2 and G-3
- **A tree's later evidence and a blind spot's count are said only at the finish.** A killed watch, or a service that
  runs for weeks, never says them. EVENT.md:82-84 says "their last is said once, at the finish", but the G-7 paragraph
  does not say that a run with no end has none of them. **[SILENCE; certain for killed runs, by ruling]**
- **Clocks.** At rev 1, `time` is the entry's own: `self.at(now)` (start wall time plus monotonic elapsed,
  host.py:1791) or `first_seen`. A later rev, a heartbeat and the end use `iso(self.wall())`, which is `time.time()`
  (host.py:1509 and 4523). The start uses `self.at(self.t0)`.
    - These are two clocks. A wall-clock step, or drift over weeks, can make a later line's `time` earlier than an
    earlier line's. Rev-1 lines also carry an older entry time by design.
  - EVENT.md names neither the clock nor the fact that `time` does not rise from line to line.

## 7. G-4: the end's word
- **Where the word comes from.** `_events_end` (host.py:1971-1981) calls `result()` and uses `result.upper()` and
  `EXIT[result]`. The report's RESULT line uses the same `result()` and `EXIT`. CLEAN, ALARM, INCOMPLETE and NONE-SEEN
  match, and so does a crash passed to `finish(status="crashed")` (ALARM). Suppressed is counted the same way on both
  sides (`overlay.suppressed(self.all_not_judged())`).
- **Where it differs.**
    - `_write_state` runs **after** the end, and its failure sets `state_error` (host.py:4153-4156 and 4616-4620),
    which `result()` turns into INCOMPLETE (host.py:4217). So the end says CLEAN exit 0 while the report says
    INCOMPLETE exit 3. **[SILENCE; low]**
  - The reverse is also possible: a `state_error` from the last round that clears at the finish. **[MORE SAID; low]**
- **When the report cannot be built.** `summary()` raises, `finish` returns "no final report" with exit 1
  (host.py:4157-4161), and the file's end still says the earlier word (for example CLEAN, exit 0). **[SILENCE; low]**
- **When `result()` or `_refresh` raises inside `_events_end`.** No end is written. The `events-failed` hole is made
  after the final pass (so it is never in the file), and only if `events_error` was still None. The file looks killed.

## 8. G-6 (a) and (b)
- **(a), deviation from "each entry in its own guard, the naming too".** In `each`, `entries[j-1]` sits outside
  `one()`'s guard (eventlog.py:223). For the knock rows that read is `Lazy` → `row()`, meaning the watch's
  `_knock_row` and `_knocked_for` (host.py:1919-1923).
    - If one row raises, the whole knock list ends at the list-level `except` (eventlog.py:235-237) and the mark is
    not advanced. Every pass, and every beat, stops at the same row.
  - **The knocks after it are never said. [SILENCE; low]**
    - That `except` appends its problem on every pass (it is not "first time only"), and the retry loop at
    host.py:1929-1936 runs the pass twice. So **two `event-not-built` holes are made each round, for good. [MORE SAID;
    low]**
- **(a), what holds.** The pass, the heartbeat and the start each have their own guard (host.py:1945-1969). A due beat
  is still written. A failing pass is retried at the next round, not at the next tick. Nothing found reaches the
  round.
- **(b), the torn check.** The check runs only while `self.whole` is false, that is, at the first write and after a
  failure (eventlog.py:129-136 and 146). G-6 (b) says "before a line is appended". A partial line appended by anyone
  else after this writer's first write (the watched agent, which THREAT_MODEL.md:159-160 says can write the file, or a
  second writer) gets the next line **glued** to it. **[MORE SAID / lost line; low-medium in an adversarial case]**
- **(b), what a reader sees.** A reader that splits on newlines sees the fragment as one non-JSON line *in the middle*
  of the file, followed by whole lines.
- **(b), when a write fails.** Nothing is recorded as said (eventlog.py:147).
  - A rev-1 entry is retried at the next pass: rev 1, a new n.
  - A later rev of an entry that is not open is retried only at the next beat or the finish: the same rev, a new n.
    - The start, the end and a failed heartbeat are never retried. The next heartbeat reuses the failed one's rev
    number. If the start fails, the first "watch" line is a heartbeat at rev 1.
    - A write whose data landed but whose flush or close raised is retried, giving **the same rev twice under two ids.
    [MORE SAID; low]**

## 11. For the builder of M10.3 (reading only docs/specs/EVENT.md)
Reading EVENT.md alone, they would still have to guess the following.
- **That the file holds many runs.** `~/.raqib/host/events.jsonl` is shared by every run on the account and only ever
  appended to.
- **Whether Raqib ever rotates or truncates the file,** and what a file deleted or replaced under them means.
- **Whether two watches can append at once.**
- **Where a run starts.** A run starts at "watch" `state: start`, but that line can be missing (a failed write), in
  which case the first "watch" line is a heartbeat at rev 1.
- **When to call a run killed.** A run ends at `state: end` or never. "Two beats" cannot be read from the file.
- **How to tell a line still being written from a torn one.** A last line without its newline may still be in
  progress, so they must wait for the newline. A torn fragment can later sit in the middle of the file as a non-JSON
  line, and the document does not say to skip it.
- **That lines are never rewritten by Raqib.** EVENT.md says "appended" but not this, and THREAT_MODEL (not EVENT.md)
  says the agent can rewrite the file.
- **How to build a cursor.** `watch.host` plus `id` dedups, but they are not told that n rises strictly in file order
  within a run, with gaps. Nor that one about and rev can appear under two ids (a write retried after its close
  failed; a heartbeat rev reused after a failed write). Nor that `time` neither rises nor comes from one clock.
- **The stale or incomplete parts.** EVENT.md:6 says the watch does not write events yet. Nothing says that `snapshot`
  cannot say `same_as`.
