## VERDICT: STOP

The two questions of a pass are asked one after the other, each with its own record bound. Membership is "as the first
  read left it", and the second read's bound is "the top of its whole answer". Together these permanently and silently
  drop a value set by any process whose start is logged after the first read's answer. Under a first-read cut that
  covers every such process past the cut. Fixing it needs a Table ruling on the order of the two questions or on what
  the second read's bound may pass (AB-3 does not settle it).

The rest is sound and in Scope:
- the pattern
- the gates on `value_name` and `program`
- the rule and its fixtures
- the watch's road through `act_key`, `judge_source` and `told_twice`
- the guard edits

## FINDINGS

**F1. HIGH. Silence, plus a false statement.**
- Where:
  - raqib/hooks/os_sysmon.py:1098-1109: the two questions are asked in sequence, each with its own bound.
  - :512 and :418-431: an event 13 is kept only if its process is already a member.
  - :1126-1129: `registry_top` becomes the highest record of the whole second answer.
- What goes wrong: a process whose start (event 1) is logged after the first wevtutil's answer, and whose value set is
  logged before the second's, is not a member. Its event 13 is dropped, and its record is passed by `registry_top`.
  The next pass's first read adds the process to the tree, but the second read asks above that record, so the value is
  never read. Nothing says so.
- reg.exe starts and sets its value within milliseconds, which is exactly the commander's act 2. Each such act falls
  into this window with a probability of roughly the window over `every`.
- When the first read reaches its cap (FOLLOW_CUT, `ROUND_CAP` 2000, for example during a large install), the second
  read runs ahead of the cut. Every value set by a process started past the cut is lost. FOLLOW_CUT's sentence (:990)
  and YAML :15 ("read by the next pass") are then false for those events 13.
- Tests: the stand-in channel grows only in `on_pass` (tests/test_registry_lines.py:275-291), between passes, so this
  case is never exercised. "A process whose start came in an earlier pass" covers only the other direction.
- Not said in THREAT_MODEL:671-731 or the YAML. THREAT_MODEL:599-600 has a general sentence, but only in the
  logged-time section.
