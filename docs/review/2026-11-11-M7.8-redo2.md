   never lets the nearer tree decide. Outer A has spent its FIRST-FINDING and reads the act in poll k: "not
   notified". Nested B's copy comes in poll k+1 and moves in with "knocked once, as a finding of tree pid A
   ...: not notified": zero knocks, no KNOCKS row, B's FIRST never spent - S1(a) verbatim. The T-1 tests put
