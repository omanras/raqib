# M14.1c REDO - questions, choices and the tests changed (ADR-0007 Amendment BZ)

What the builder did for R1 to R12, each choice where the HANDOFF was silent,
and each existing test changed, with why. Nothing here is answered locally;
the questions are for the Table.

## The items

- R1 (BZ-1). `_act_roots` skips a root none of whose records is a sampled
  one (`not any(not _reader_src(r) for r in p["recs"])`): one condition after
  the existing ones, so an act-root of sampled processes is built as before,
  to the byte. Test: a sampled terminal (gnome-terminal-server) and its bash,
  under which the reader alone gives tool -> sh -> git: no act-root and no
  tree; the same chain in the sample is an act-root (pid 2020).
- R2 (BZ-2). Where the reader answered at least once and was not whole, the
  note is `BETWEEN_HEARD` + "; the reader was not whole for the whole watch:
  <why>" (`BETWEEN_NOT_WHOLE`); "the rounds read /proc only ..." is said only
  where no ask was answered. Tests: one late round after two answered ones;
  two late rounds and no answer.
- R3 (BZ-3). The words of the section (`PE_JUDGED`), the note
  (`BETWEEN_HEARD`), GUIDE, THREAT_MODEL and PROC-EVENTS name as not judged:
  a program a process changes to after its line was written and the programs
  it had before; a child whose parent was given in an earlier round and ended
  before the sample; and say a child is named by its program's file (a link's
  target), not the name it was called by. "A program not read ... in no
  round" is now "a program not read is counted, with no line". Tests hold the
  sentences (tests/test_procevents_round.py, the two E6 tests).
- R4 (BZ-4). `discover.reader_time` reads a reader's time and never raises
  (ValueError, OverflowError, TypeError -> None). `parse_procevents` and the
  watch's `_pe_given` make a line not a line where `began` or `ended` is not
  readable, and where the pid or the parent pid is at or above
  `discover.REUSE`. Tests: through the parser and through the watch (the
  round is kept as the sample's alone, the hole is "not a line"), for
  9999-12-31T23:59:59-01:00 (OverflowError), year 0000, "soon", pid and ppid
  10**10.
- R5 (BZ-5). `_reader_instances`: a record without ticks joins the sampled
  process of its pid only where the sample read no start for it; the join by
  the fork's time is gone. A record with ticks still joins where the ticks
  are the same, or where the sample read no start (as the merge by pid was).
- R6 (BZ-6). An answered round whose answer holds back a rest (`more`) is a
  hole of the result of the kind `procevents` (`PE_MORE`), said once an
  episode (an episode: answered rounds with `more` running).
- R7 (BZ-7). The live reader keeps its copies of the watch's record
  (`own["mine"]`: pid -> when recorded, image, the round it was copied in)
  until the answer that covers that round was taken: ProcEvents counts its
  rounds, notes which round wrote each ask (from the client's `asks`, the
  last few), and, on an answer that holds back no rest, `covered` is the
  round its ask was written in; copies of rounds up to `covered` are then
  dropped. At most 1024 copies (`PE_OWN_MAX`), the oldest forgotten. The
  count of the watch's own processes left out is `inv["procevents"]["own"]`
  and a line of the section where it is not 0. A stand-in helper that counts
  no ask: its answer covers its own round.
- R8 (BZ-8). The live reader does not ask `proc_start_times` for a child
  that only the reader gave (every cite of its act is of the process events)
  and that the reader said ended (every record of that pid in the answer has
  an end); its start in `child_started` is the record's `began`. THREAT_MODEL
  now says that the watch reads /proc/PID/stat for the start of each other
  child the round names.
- R9 (BZ-9). The full-input test's stand-in fills the pipe, then makes its
  writing end block again, and the round is asked in a thread joined for
  3 s. Shown failing: with `os.set_blocking(fd, False)` in `Helper.start`
  replaced by `pass`, the test failed ("the ask's write blocked on a full
  input": the thread was alive after 3 s); the line was put back and it
  passed. That test and the new one of an input that cannot be made
  unblocking are skipped off Linux by name (they need os.set_blocking on a
  pipe).
- R10 (BZ-10). The stand-in's wget has `--tries=1`. X36 now reads the
  watch's source files (`--sources`, already given by the Linux rig): MET
  only where a NET-TOOL line of the stand-in's tree names wget and a
  HostChild line of that tree (`input_meta.pid` the stand-in's pid, wget
  among `command_programs`) cites `procevents`; MISSED with "the sample's
  own" where every such line cites the sample alone; MISSED where no such
  line is found ("whose word it is cannot be told", also with no sources).
- R11 (BZ-11, BZ-12). `PE_GAP` says "a hole of the result", not "the result
  is incomplete"; GUIDE and PROC-EVENTS say INCOMPLETE "unless a finding makes
  it an alarm". `RHYTHM_HOLES_NOTE`, the evasion lines of NET-TOOL,
  DESTRUCT-CMD and HOST-RHYTHM, and raqib/ruleform.py's host-source sentence
  name the reader beside Sysmon (words only: no match, no number).
  THREAT_MODEL says a tree's line holds the reader's record itself, its path
  included, made printable and cut as a sampled child's is. `run_ss` kills
  its ss on any error of its reading, not only at its timeout. A helper
  input that has a descriptor and cannot be made unblocking is the helper
  not started, said ("the helper's input could not be made unblocking (...)");
  an input with no descriptor (a test's stand-in) is written as before.
- R12. The gate (below).

## Choices where the HANDOFF was silent

- C1 (R7). Which answer covers a copy: the one whose ask was written in a
  round no earlier than the copy's, with no rest held back. A copy is made
  before the sample and after it, each round, so the copy of a socket tool
  run after a round's build is made in the next round, before the ask that
  will tell of it.
- C2 (R8). A pid given twice in one answer is not read in /proc only where
  every record of it has an end; the start is the first record's `began`.
- C3 (R6). The hole's words: "more: the answer held back a rest for a next
  one: a child among it is judged a round late, and one whose parent has left
  by then is in no tree".
- C4 (R10). X36's "the sample's own" is MISSED, not OPEN (the rig asked for
  the reader's road and did not see it). Q1: should it be OPEN?
- C5 (R10). A HostChild line names several children together and its cites
  are those of all of them; X36 takes the line as the reader's where any of
  its cites is of the process events. Q2: is a per-child source wanted on
  the line (the children's entries carry no cite)?
- C6 (R4). Only the processes of an answer are held to a readable time (the
  round reads no other); `ends` and `older` are counted as before.
- C7 (R11). NEW-AGENT.yaml and NEW-AGENT-UNKNOWN.yaml hold the same stale
  sentence on a child between two rounds; they are not among the three rules
  the HANDOFF names and are left. Q3.

## Existing tests changed, and why

- tests/test_host_procevents.py (in Scope):
  - `rec`'s made-up end times "E" and "F" are now readable times (BZ-4 makes
    an unreadable end not a line); the five uses keep their assertions.
  - `test_e3_one_helper_...`: the round's dict gains `"own": 0` (BZ-7).
  - `test_e9_...`: the param with `more=True` now adds 3 holes of the result,
    not 2 (BZ-6).
  - `test_e3_where_the_reader_was_not_healthy_...`: where any ask was answered
    the note is BETWEEN_HEARD with the reason (BZ-2); the refused param keeps
    the old words.
  - `test_e6_the_documents_say_...`: the held threat-model sentence says
    "reaches a tree's programs", not "a tree's line" (BZ-11).
- tests/test_procevents_round.py (in Scope): the join by the fork's time
  replaced by BZ-5's test; the full-input test (BZ-9); the E6 words.
- tests/test_rig_check.py (in Scope): the X36 tests give source lines.
- tests/test_discover.py (holds the count): the guard's list of calls in
  `run_ss` is now Popen, kill, kill (BZ-12).
- tests/test_readme_top.py (holds GUIDE's sentences word for word): its
  `PROCESS_EVENTS` list is the guide's new paragraph.

No Windows test was edited; no rule's fixture changed; no fixture's finding
changed.
