# M2.2 — questions and readings taken

1. **A round page without a round GET.** The server has no `GET .../rounds/:n`
   and server/ is out of this Scope. Reading taken: the round page and the
   rounds list read `GET /api/circles/:id/statement`, which already carries
   every round with its lines (state, amounts, reports, seqs, contribution
   ids), payout and status, and every member may read it. No route was
   added.

2. **Who sees which tools.** Settings and Activity tabs are for the
   organizer (the server answers 403 to members anyway). The member sees
   "Report my payment" on the Overview when their line in the open round
   still has something reportable, and "I received this payout" when they
   are the recipient and a payout waits for acknowledgement. The organizer
   can record a payment for any member, confirm pending reports, waive
   with a reason, open and close rounds, and record the payout.

3. **Two steps for what cannot be undone.** Start, cancel, leave, and
   closing a round with outstanding payments each need a second click on a
   button that names the consequence; closing a round with nothing
   outstanding is one click.

4. **Manual order** uses up/down buttons (labelled "Move <name> up/down")
   rather than drag-and-drop: it works with a keyboard, a screen reader and
   a phone. The handoff allows either.

5. **Mixed-direction sentences.** Amounts, dates, logins and request ids
   inside translated sentences are inserted as `<bdi dir="ltr">` elements
   (`tx()` in client/i18n), so Arabic word order around them stays right;
   a first version forced whole sentences LTR and scrambled them (seen in
   screenshots and fixed).

6. **Payout amount default.** The payout form starts at the confirmed pot
   and follows it when confirmations arrive (the form is re-created when
   the confirmed total changes); the organizer types what was actually
   paid, and a difference from the confirmed pot is shown.

7. **Share this round** copies plain text (circle, round n of N,
   recipient, due date with days left or overdue, confirmed of expected,
   one line per member with their state and lateness) in the current
   language, for pasting into a messaging app. Nothing is sent anywhere by
   Dawr.

8. **Circle created -> Members tab.** After creating a circle the organizer
   lands on Members, where the invitation links are made.

9. **Draw link.** The "run a draw instead" link and the Draw tab arrive with
   the Draw screen in M2.3, so no link in M2.2 points at a screen that does
   not exist yet.
