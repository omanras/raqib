## Questions

1. A missing severity, or one the contract does not have. Y-2's table names high, medium, a hole, low, info and
   none. Built: anything else, or no severity at all, is Warning (the syslog rule "never calm", J-6's
   SEVERITY_ELSE); a kind that is none of the eight, or none, is 1000 as Y-2 says. Is Warning right?
2. The id in a part's head. Y-3 says "an id that is no text of at most 128 printable characters is written as
   `-`". Built: printable ASCII with no space (`[\x21-\x7e]{1,128}`), since the head is split at its spaces
   and a non-ASCII character may be two UTF-16 units (the head must stay within 300). An id with a space, or
   with a printable non-ASCII character, is therefore `-`. Is that the ruling's meaning?
3. Characters and UTF-16 units. The parts are cut at 30000 characters of the line. An event's line is ASCII
   (EVENT.md; classify reads ASCII only), so a character is one UTF-16 unit and a part is far inside 32731. A
   line that is not ASCII is never sent (it is not an event). Recorded, in case a later shape sends other
   lines.
4. A reader of parts after a refusal. When a part is refused, the batch is handed again from its first line, so
   the log may hold `1/N`, `2/N` and then `1/N` ... `N/N`. Built and documented: a part 1 begins the line anew
   (tools/rig/check.py and FORWARD.md say so). Should FORWARD.md give the reader a fuller rule (for example,
   group by sha256)?
5. The process id the rig reads back. X27 asks wevtutil for the records whose Execution ProcessID is the
   forwarder's pid as its own state says it (`pid`), not the pid of the process the rig started: on Windows a
   venv's python.exe may be a launcher whose child is the forwarder. Both are recorded in the facts
   (`pid`, `process`). Is the state's pid an acceptable word here (X-7: the agent can rewrite that file)?
6. The made-up long file of X27 holds three lines, the second of exactly 100000 characters (four parts), so
   that the parts are seen in their place between whole lines. E7 says "one line of 100000 characters"; if the
   Table meant a file of that one line only, it is a one-line change in forward_run.py.
7. The event's Level as wevtutil prints it. The rig's made-up system writes Level 2, 3, 4 for Error, Warning,
   Information; X27 does not judge the Level. To be checked in the field.
8. `--facility` with `eventlog:` is refused even as `--facility 13` (Y-5 names --facility among what is not
   taken). Recorded.
