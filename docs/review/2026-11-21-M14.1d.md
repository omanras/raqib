# Review packet: M14.1d and its REDO (bf47ca6, c260c0e)
VERDICT: STOP   (a recommendation: rulings first, then a short REDO of the items below)
Reading the code, the mechanism the rig measured works. A heard process's programs reach its line, the kernel's
name goes through the gate, kept children hold a tree's child across its parent's end, and CD-1 makes the outliving
bash its maker's child. But the REDO's quiet-tree road in host.py, which X45 rests on, answers the first build's Q3
locally and has no test. X43 and X45 can be MET by another child's wget. CD-1 lets the reader's link make an
act-root no sample made, and lets it take a sampled child out of its tree. The kernel's name alone now makes a line
where BY-4 says none. The threat model and the spec still state the M14.1c silences that M14.1d closed.

## MUST FIX BEFORE MERGE
1. tools/rig/check.py:1151-1153, 1147-1149, 1172-1176 (X43, X45). Verified by reading.
   - What is wrong: the row is MET where any HostChild line of the stand-in's tree has "wget" anywhere in
     command_programs and the pid anywhere in children, and any NET-TOOL line on the stand-in names wget.
   - Why it misleads: a tree gets one line per round, naming all its children's new programs together. The outliving
     bash's first line (dash, sh, bash, sleep) can share a round with X43's wget; they start 1-3 s apart and rounds

     bash's fetch was never said. X43 can be MET the same way by X36's wget, and X36's NET-TOOL line alone satisfies
     the NET-TOOL half of both rows.

     name, host.py:4877), and a NET-TOOL line at that HostChild line's seq. Add a test with a shared line; there is
     none.
