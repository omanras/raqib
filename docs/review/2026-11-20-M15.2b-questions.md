The builder's choices where the HANDOFF was silent, each the smallest thing that met the exit
criterion, and the existing tests that had to change because they held, word for word or by count,

## Choices

- Q-1 (E1: the fixture's registration). The fixture of an unread Initiated is a new file,
  `tests/fixtures/rules/NET-ADMIN-PORT/negative-initiated-unread.json` (by the generator: a private
  22 with no Initiated, a private 3389 with an empty one, a private 445 with "maybe", and a public
  22 with none, which is judged false whatever it was and so is not in the note). It is listed in
  `NET-ADMIN-PORT.yaml`'s `fixtures: negative:`, one line beside the evasion line and the
  description words the scope names: without it tests/test_rules.py's
  `test_e4_every_rule_fixture_file_is_referenced` fails. Is that line in the scope as the Table meant it?
- Q-2 (E1: negative-not-admin.json). Its 22 whose Initiated is not read (the line that held
  `initiated: null`) moved to the new file. Left there with no key, it would make that file
  unmeasured (raqib/rules/fixtures.py `outcome`: a line the rule cannot judge leaves the file
  unmeasured), NET-ADMIN-PORT would have no measured negative and `raqib check-rules` would say
  `unmeasured`. Its bytes change (one net line fewer, the seqs after it), its findings do not (none),
  and it is still measured. NET-ADMIN-PORT's unmeasured negatives go from 9 to 10. `raqib check-rules`
  after it:

      NET-ADMIN-PORT   ok               0.000    0.05      1 positive; negatives incl. benign: 1 measured, 10 unmeasured (missing context or unjudgeable lines)

- Q-3 (E1: lines already written). A line an earlier build wrote with `"initiated": null` is still a
  value to the engine: judged "not initiated", and not said. The engine is not touched here (E5).
  Should such old lines be said, and where?
- Q-4 (E3: how a stand-in is never asked). The question goes through `read_live_bounded.newest`,
  which is `read_live_bounded` itself (set once in os_sysmon). The tests' stand-ins of the round's
  read (`FakeWevtutil`, the third field run's `Channel`, the lambdas) replace `read_live_bounded`
  and have no `newest`, so `Channel.registry_held` returns None: nothing is asked, nothing is said,
  and the inventory carries no `registry`. A `Channel` given a reader of its own is asked only
  through that reader's `newest`. The live reader also asks only where the channel has the method.
- Q-5 (E3: the question's form). `wevtutil qe Microsoft-Windows-Sysmon/Operational
  /q:*[System[(EventID=13)]] /rd:true /c:1 /f:xml`: the newest event 13 of the whole channel, no time
  bound (the HANDOFF says "the channel's newest event 13"). An answer whose time cannot be read is
  `{"held": true, "newest": null}`.
- Q-6 (E3: when it is asked). After the round's inventory is built (after its read, discover's run,
  the silence check and the commit), in the first round whose read had no error. A reader that fails
  between its read and its inventory asks nothing that round; the next round that reads asks. A
  channel closed by its owner answers `{"error": "the channel is closed (its watch stopped): not
  asked"}`, which the watch says as not known.
- Q-7 (E3: where the answer is carried). Only in `inv["channel"]["registry"]` of the round it was
  asked in, not in every later round's record.
- Q-8 (E3: how it is said). Through the watch's `_blind` (kind `registry`): in the state, the page
  and the report's blind spots, counted apart, "(does not change the result)"; it is not printed on
  the terminal when it is made, as no other blind spot is. The hole kind `registry` (the watches'
  registry row) is another list. The two sentences do not start with the kind's word (as
  WIN_BYTES_HOLE and STARTED_UNREAD), since the report prints `  - registry: <sentence>`.
- Q-9 (E2: the sentence and its place). CHANNEL_HELD is said in order of the event id, so event 11's
  sentence comes between event 3's and event 22's in the notes and in the report's CHANNEL line.
- Q-10 (E4: two older sentences). The guide's "(no event 1, 3 or 22) is said once" and the threat
  model's "a kind of event 1, 3 or 22 not read after three rounds" now name 11 too; left as they
  were they would be untrue.
- Q-11 (E4: NET-ADMIN-PORT's row). Besides the unread Initiated it says what the watch already said
  of event 3 (CHANNEL_HELD[3] is as it was; only the row says it now).
