## Questions (each: what the code does meanwhile)
1. `drive_refused` lost its third argument `path`, which fed only the clause Q-2 takes out. Meanwhile: two arguments;
   `_Walk._drive` and the tests call it so.
2. The head now names "(ADR-0013 P-1, Q-1)", and `DRIVE_ONLY` and `drives_said` (the pass's end, after "to the
   drive's letter") say "of the form \Device\HarddiskVolumeN" in place of "beginning \Device\". X49's and X50's
   patterns stop before them (`test_x49_and_x50_still_read_the_pass_with_its_drives` passes). Is that the Table's
   wording?
3. tools/rig/check.py: besides X51's reading and its constants, its module docstring's X51 lines (71-75) said "does
   not begin with \Device\"; they are changed to the form. `SERVICE_CHECK_DRIVE_NAME` is unchanged ("a device's name
   that is not a disk's"). Is the docstring within the scope's "X51's reading"?
4. A letter given to a disk's own root (its device's name `\??\C:\`) is refused by its form, though what is above it
   is a disk's root. Refused rather than taken; the Table may rule otherwise after measuring.
5. E5, a search call's name in a string: every string and bytes constant of raqib/ that holds a name of SEARCH,
   ENV_SET (SetEnvironmentVariableW/A, putenv) or LOW (`_dlopen`, `LibraryLoader`) is a fault, but a docstring or any
   bare string statement (words, never a value), and `ALLOWED_WORDS` = own.py's `library_folder`, whose result words
   say "SetDllDirectoryW(NULL) ..." (four strings). That is the strictest the real tree passes; own.py is outside
   Scope. Should those words be reworded so no allowed place is needed?
6. E5, the environment: `os.environ[...] = ...` and `os.environ.setdefault(...)` are read by key, as before (the
   tree's HOME and USERPROFILE); `update`, `|=`, `os.environ = ...`, `os.putenv` and SetEnvironmentVariableW/A are
   faults (the keys still read, so PATH is still named). Should setdefault be refused too? The tree does not use it.
7. E5, beyond its list and kept because the real tree passes it: a class, loader or finder of ctypes' met other than
   as what a call calls (`k = ctypes.WinDLL`, a subclass, `functools.partial(ctypes.CDLL)`, `w = ctypes.windll`) is a
   fault. And a table is "changed" by more than E5's list: a second binding of its name, `global` of it, and the
   methods add, pop, popitem, remove, discard, clear, sort, reverse and the dunder writers (CHANGERS). Keep?
8. E5, a loader handed on: `ALLOWED_HANDED` is empty - the tree hands no loader on. A lambda's own binding line
   (`loader = loader or (lambda ...)`) is not counted as handing it on.
9. E5, CI-3's road: held for the function that holds the call (one parameter; the call read once and called once,
   given that parameter), its name (met once in raqib/, the one argument of `library_folder(...)`) and own.py's
   `library_folder` (its first parameter called once, with None, and not met otherwise). `Scan.ci3` says both files
   were read, and the real-tree test holds it. `test_p2_the_one_search_call_is_cis_...` (by text) is kept as it was.
10. GUIDE.md now carries "The drive is read with the check's own reader of its device's name; the autostart reader
    also asks a drive's kind, for its own purpose." E6 names the three documents together; is this sentence wanted in
    the guide, or only in SERVICE.md and THREAT_MODEL.md?
11. Finding 12 (earlier tests pass their drive on DISK_STAND_IN, not measured) is kept as Q-6 says; the stand-in is
    of Q-1's form and `test_p1_a_second_drive_that_is_a_disk_...` holds that it is.

## What the libraries' scan still does not see (also in its docstring)
A call's name built from pieces at run time and given to getattr of an object that is not ctypes' own; a table
changed by a function it is handed to; a module of ctypes bound to an attribute (`api.ctypes.WinDLL`) or by
assignment, and what is loaded through it; exec, eval, importlib; loads the interpreter itself makes, outside raqib/
(ctypes' own windll.kernel32 at its import, _ctypes' dependencies, tkinter's libraries); folders the interpreter's own
start adds (site, a *.pth line, sitecustomize); whether a library's own dependencies are searched as the library is.
