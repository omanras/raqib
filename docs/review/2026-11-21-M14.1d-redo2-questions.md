## The items

- R1 (CE-1). The quiet-tree road goes the held trees' road.
  - `_attach_orphans` finds the acts for a tree that is not in the round's inventory.
  - `_give_kept`, called once the round knows its quiet and kept trees, gives them by AY-2's road:
    - only where `_host_unwritable` lets the tree be written;
    - marked in `given_marks`, with the word "none: the process events (a child of a child it kept)" as the held
      tree's line's `inventory_tree`;
    - else the hole `held-not-given`, once a tree.
  - Their `dropped` is counted with the inventory's.
  - In the held trees' loop a kept child given again counts as gained only where it ran a name its lines have not
    said. With nothing new, there is no line, and no tree line for it either.
  - A tree that has gone is written nothing. The processes its kept children were heard to start are counted
    (`PE_GONE_KEPT` in the section) and are one hole of the result (kind `procevents`) a tree.
  - Tests: the tree quiet in the round of the fetch (the wget's line in round 3, NET-TOOL once, the AZ-3 mark); a
    kept child given again with nothing new (no HostChild line and no HostTree line in round 3); the tree gone in
    that round (no line, one hole, the section's count).
  - Shown failing: with the call to `_give_kept` replaced by `pass`, the quiet test and the gone test failed. With
    the new-name condition of the held loop taken out, the nothing-new test failed. Both were put back and pass.
- R2 (CE-2).
  - In `_merge_procs` the reader's parent stands only where it is a process of the round (its sample or its text).
    A sampled process whose parent it changes keeps the sample's own as `sampled_ppid`.
  - `_act_roots` reads a view of the round in which those processes have the sample's parent again, their link
    dated afresh (`_sample_links`). The reader's processes alone keep the reader's links, so a chain the reader
    alone gave under a sampled act-root still adds its acts (CB-1).
  - Tests: the review's case 5 (no act-root, as the samples alone make none); a sampled child given again whose
    reader's parent is in neither sample nor text stays its tree's act. The field's case (CD-1's tests) passes.
- R3 (CE-3). A child the process events alone gave, whose file was not read and whose kernel name was, is named by
  that name, with its `command_sources`. Its line carries `input_meta.files_unread` (those names).
  - Such children are counted in the section (`PE_ALONE`) and taken out of "with no program read (no line)".
  - Test: one named `wget` (NET-TOOL fires; `files_unread: ["wget"]`), and one with neither file nor name (no line,

## Readings taken

- Q1 (CE-2, "a child the watch keeps"). discover does not know the watch's kept children. So the reader's parent
  stands in discover only where it is a process of the round.
  - Where it is a kept child (its parent in neither sample nor text), the sample's parent stands in the inventory,
    and the watch gives the process to the kept parent's tree only where no tree of the round took it.
  - A sampled process the sample puts under another talking tree (a subreaper) stays that tree's, though its
    reader's parent is a kept child. Should the watch take it from there?
- Q2 (CE-1, "the hole where one is held and not given"). Read as AZ-2's own hole, `held-not-given`, once a tree,
  with its words naming the kept children.
- Q3 (CE-1, a gone tree). Read as: a tree in neither the round's trees, its quiet ones nor its kept ones. The count
  is of the acts found for it; a later round adds to the count, and the hole is said once a tree.
- Q4 (CE-3, "a word in the line"). Read as a key of the line's input_meta, `files_unread`, holding the names of
  children whose file was not read. The line's programs and sources are as for any child. No rule reads the key.

## Choices where the HANDOFF was silent

- C1. A kept child whose tree is in the inventory under another key form (`_one_key` merging two keys of one tree)
  is treated as not in the inventory. On Linux the key's start is boot ticks and does not change.
- C2. `PE_ALONE` counts distinct (tree, child) pairs over the watch; "no line" is the round counts less that number.
- C3. X46's stand-in says the pid sudo has, not the fetch's; X46 is read by the line's own facts, not by that pid.
- C4. The stand-in now waits for a killed child, so its rc is said (the review's cosmetic note).
