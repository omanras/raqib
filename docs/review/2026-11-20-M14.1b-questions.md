# M14.1b - questions and the choices made where the HANDOFF was silent

ADR-0007 Amendment BU (BU-1 to BU-5). Each choice below is the smallest the builder could find; each is
the Table's to confirm or to rule otherwise.

## Choices

1. **A hole of the helper is a blind spot, not a hole of the result.** E3 calls a refusal, a late answer
   and an end "a hole of a kind of its own"; BU-3 says the result and the exit code are what they were.
   A `_hole` of the watch makes the result INCOMPLETE (exit 3), so each is a hole of the class "blind
   spot" (`_blind`, K-8: counted apart, never exit 3), of the kinds `process events refused`, `not
   started`, `late`, `not a line`, `ended`, `ended for good`. The blind list holds each kind once with
   the rounds it lasted; the terminal says `BLIND SPOT round N: the process events: ...` once at the
   start of each episode (an episode ends at a round without that kind).
2. **The answer carries the ask's number.** The client sends `read N`; the answer has `"ask": N`
   (`null` for a bare `read`), a key beside E1's five. An answer whose number is lower than the ask's is
   left and counted (`Helper.stale`); a higher one is `not a line`.
3. **What the helper holds is bounded by the Reader's own bounds.** A new `Reader.read()` is taken only
   when what the helper holds from the reads before was given whole (SERVE_MAX 2000 per answer,
   processes, then ends, then older).
4. **`refused` is a fourth reason of `ask()`** (E2 names `late`, `ended`, `not a line`): the refusal of
   the ready line, its words in `Helper.refused`.
5. **The helper starts before the first round's sample**, not after it: it is ready when asked after
   the sample (the reader's start may wait 2 s for the kernel), and it is in that first sample as the
   watch's own.
6. **The record's image is the interpreter's real file.** `own.started(proc)` records `sys.executable`'s
   basename (`python3`); a /proc sample reads the exe link (`python3.12`), so BI-2 would not prove it.
   Where they differ, host.helper_started also calls `own.RECORD.add(pid, image=realpath)`.
7. **The helper's process:** standard error to nothing (a crash is said as `ended`); its directory the
   one that holds the raqib package the watch runs (so `-m` finds that raqib without `-I`); the
   environment inherited; a Ctrl-C at the terminal ignored (main() only; the end of input ends it);
   exit 1 where its output cannot be written, 2 for a usage other than `--serve`.
8. **What is counted.** `began`: the records whose fork was heard (`began` not null), each counted once
   by (pid, its fork's time) - a process given again after another exec is not counted twice; a bounded
   memory of 32768 keys, the oldest forgotten (a forgotten key could then be counted again). Threads,
   and processes whose fork was not heard, are not counted. `inside`: those of them whose end came in
   the same answer. `unread_exe`: the step of the reader's `missed.exe` (reads, not processes). A
   process forked and never heard to exec counts as began, not as a program not read.
9. **A reader that ended inside the helper** (its counts' `ended`) is taken as the helper's end:
   stopped and started again once; a second is final.
10. **A first start that fails is final** (`not started`), as a refusal is: neither is started again.
11. **Healthy for the whole watch** = every round answered with no hole and nothing lost. The Linux note
    is replaced in place in the watch's notes (report and state); the watch's first lines, said before
    any round, are unchanged.
12. **`rounds asked`** counts the rounds whose inventory carried the key, rounds after a final hole
    included; `answered` the rounds with no hole.
13. **The helper's stop problems at the watch's stop are a note**, not a hole (the result unchanged).
    A single look's close returns them as `the process events' helper: ...`, which app.py prefixes with
    `the look's channel:`.
14. **A client let go without stop()** closes its helper's input (`Helper.__del__`): subprocess keeps a
    running child's Popen alive to reap it, so the pipe would not close by itself. Existing tests that
    call `live_reader(platform="linux")` on a Linux machine now start a real helper; it ends when the
    reader is let go.
15. **X35 reads the report and the workload** (the stand-in's `start` lines) and is a row of main()
    always, not of read(); no argument is added and rig.yml is unchanged. Holes said in the section do
    not make it MISSED; they are in its words.
16. **THREAT_MODEL.md**: besides the new bullets, the words "Linux (no audit source of process events
    is read)" became "Linux (its process events are counted, not judged)" in the paragraph on what
    lives between two rounds; "keep the whole hole" stands.
