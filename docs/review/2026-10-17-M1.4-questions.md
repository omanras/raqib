# M1.4 — questions and readings taken

1. **Totals from the ledger, not from tables.** Statements, the overview,
   the circle summary and the "My circles" badges are computed by
   `shared/books.ts foldBooks`, a pure fold over the circle's ledger
   entries (the browser can run the same code on an export). The DB tables
   (`rounds.status`, `contributions` waiver columns, `payouts`) are indexes
   written in the same transaction as each entry and used to validate
   requests; a test checks they agree with the fold. No reports table was
   added: reports and confirmations live only in the ledger.

2. **What `:cid` is.** The id of the (round, member) obligation row in
   `contributions` (created at start). Reports are ledger entries with
   their own `reportId`. `confirm` confirms every pending report of the
   obligation, or only the `reportIds` given; `waive` waives the
   obligation (reason required) and is refused once it is fully confirmed.

3. **When money can be recorded.** Reports, confirmations, waivers and
   payouts are accepted in open *and closed* rounds (arrears are paid
   late; a closed round lists what was missing, and the money that comes
   later must be recordable), and in active or completed circles; never in
   an upcoming round (nothing is due yet; paying ahead is refused) or a
   draft/drawing/cancelled circle. Most restrictive reading that still
   lets arrears be settled.

4. **Over-payment** is measured against everything reported (confirmed or
   not): a member cannot report more than expected minus already reported
   (409 `overpayment` with the reportable amount).

5. **Reported is not paid (A5).** An obligation reported in full but not
   confirmed stays `reported` and its amount stays outstanding; only
   confirmation reduces what is owed. Arrears list such lines too.

6. **Round 1 at start.** Opens in the start transaction when the start
   date is today (UTC) or earlier; otherwise the organizer opens it. The
   last round's close completes the circle (`circle.completed` with
   confirmed and outstanding totals).

7. **Payout.** One per round (also a UNIQUE index), recorded by the
   organizer in an open or closed round with the amount actually paid, the
   date (not in the future), method and reference; the entry carries the
   confirmed pot at that moment so a difference is visible. Only the
   recipient of that round may acknowledge, once.

8. **Statement of one member.** `?member=` is allowed for a member's own
   id; another member's id is 403 (own circle, so informative); an id not
   in the circle is 404; the organizer may fetch anyone's. Lines cite the
   seqs of every report, confirmation and waiver they rest on; rounds cite
   their open, close, payout and acknowledgement seqs. CSV: UTF-8 with a
   BOM (Arabic names in spreadsheets), RFC 4180 quoting, cells starting
   with = + - @ tab or CR prefixed with a quote (S4.4), amounts as decimal
   text in the currency's minor unit.

9. **Ledger GET.** `?from=` filters the entries returned; `?verify=1`
   always verifies the whole chain from seq 1 (a partial check would not
   prove anything about the part not returned). The response carries
   `fetchedAt` so the client can show how fresh it is (A10).

10. **Audit GET.** Organizer only, newest first, `?limit=` 1-200 (default
    50) and `?before=<audit id>` for the next page; actor names are the
    members' display names in this circle.

11. **Audit coverage (S6.1).** `tests/api/audit.coverage.test.ts` runs every
    mutating route of the matrix once with a role that may use it and
    asserts an audit row with that request's id; it also fails if any
    registered mutating route is missing from the tour.
