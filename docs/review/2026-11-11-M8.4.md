# M8.4 - the window did not open on the commander's machine (the third field run's first minute)

The Table, 2026-11-11. The Table's own diagnosis, fix and tests; no builder and no fresh reviewer yet (said in
ADR-0009 Amendment I). Nothing of the commander's machine is copied here but the shape of one stack.

## What the commander saw
main 082aad6, installed on his Windows machine, Python 3.14: `raqib-app` shows a white window, "Not Responding".

## What his stack dump showed
At the Table's request he ran the window under `faulthandler.dump_traceback_later(30)`. Thirty seconds after
the start: the tray's thread in its message pump (as it should be), and the main thread in tkinter's
`update_idletasks`, called from `Window._title_bar`, from `Window.__init__`, from `app.run` - Tk's first idle
pass, the first time the window's geometry and pictures are worked out. No reader thread was out: the host was
not the cause.

## What was measured
The Table could not reproduce a hang on Linux, but could measure a cost that grows with the display's scale. A
probe branch (claude/probe-hang, a55cf6f, never merged) opened the window on windows-latest at each scale and
failed on purpose with its timings (CI run 43029531607, Python 3.11):

| scale | built (s) | first update (s) | the window white for about |
|---|---|---|---|
| the real app, 1.0 | 10.9 | 18.5 | 29 s |
| 1.0 | 10.5 | 8.8 | 19 s |
| 1.25 | 12.4 | 10.2 | 23 s |
| 1.5 | 17.7 | 14.5 | 32 s |
| 1.75 | 11.4 | 18.0 | 29 s |
| 2.0 | 14.9 | 23.6 | 38 s |
| 2.5 | 23.4 | 37.3 | 61 s |
| 3.0 | 33.7 | 53.8 | 87 s |

The same on Linux (Xvfb, a fast machine): 3.2 s at 1.0, 5.7 s at 2.0, 13.2 s at 3.0. So it was never an endless
loop: the window would have opened after half a minute to a minute and a half, and frozen again at each full
redraw. The Windows jobs of CI had always passed, because a test builds the window and never waits to see it.

## The cause
M8.1 draws every card, table, field, button and pill as a small picture and gives it to ttk as an image
element with a border: "its corners stay, its middle stretches". ttk does not stretch a middle, it TILES it,
one blit per tile, and each picture was drawn as small as its corners allowed: a middle of two pixels. A card
the size of the window was some hundred thousand blits a redraw. A profile at scale 2.0 on Linux: 13.5 s to
open, 9.7 s of it inside Tk, 3.2 s in the pure-Python painter (`_cover_rect`, sampling every edge pixel 16
times).

## The fix
- `Window._wide`: each picture of an element with a border is given a middle of THEME's `tile` (64 px at
  scale 1.0), built by Tk's own photo copy (the corners as they are, the edges and the middle tiled in C). The
  element asks the room its drawn picture asked (`width`, `height`), so no widget grows.
- `_cover_rect`: a pixel wholly inside one corner's quarter is decided by its farthest and nearest points to
  the corner's centre; only a pixel the arc crosses is sampled.
- Tests (tests/test_app_tile.py): the painter gives every pixel exactly the value sampling gave (over 250,000
  pixels of boxes with whole and fractional edges and every kind of radius); a pixel the arc does not cross is
  not sampled; every stretched picture's middle is the tile in each direction it has one, and the whole window
  as one card is at most 300 blits; the widened card is the same picture (corners pixel for pixel, edges
  repeated, the middle's colour); a button and a pill keep their size; an element without a border is given
  its picture as drawn.

## What was measured after
Linux (the same probe): 0.5 s at 1.0, 0.9 s at 2.0, 1.4 s at 3.0; the first update 0.02-0.06 s. Screenshots of
the host screen, the rules and the form, light and dark, before and after, compared pixel by pixel: the rules
and the form are identical; the host screen differs only in the clock digits of "first seen". Whole suite with
Tk 3642 passed; gate GREEN, 3582 passed.

Windows, the same probe on top of the fix (claude/probe-after, 96f5bf5, never merged; CI run 43033937244), with
the run before it beside each (Python 3.11 / 3.12; "white for" is built plus the first update):

| scale | before the fix | after the fix |
|---|---|---|
| the real app, 1.0 | 29 s / 40 s | 1.0 s / 1.4 s |
| 1.0 | 19 s / 26 s | 0.5 s / 0.7 s |
| 1.5 | 32 s / 44 s | 0.7 s / 0.9 s |
| 2.0 | 38 s / 52 s | 0.8 s / 1.1 s |
| 3.0 | 87 s / 118 s | 1.4 s / 2.0 s |

After the first update every later one is 0.016 s (one frame). CI for the fix itself (5a2aa26, run 43033903548):
six jobs success.

## What this does not answer
- Python 3.14 is what the commander runs; CI runs 3.11 and 3.12. Nothing found points at 3.14, and nothing
  tested says it is fine.
- (Answered by the probe after the fix: "built" fell with the rest, to about a second at scale 2.0 on
  Windows; the ten seconds were the first idle pass inside Window.__init__, the same tiling.)
- The window's other screens at his display's scale, the dark title bar, the open panel on a visible window:
  still his to see.
