# M1.2 routine and decisions - design questions

Readings taken by the builder where ADR-0001 sections 7-8 or the handoff
left room. Each is the most conservative reading available.

1. **`routine --period` and a policy of another period kind.** A monthly
   routine asked over a quarter or a year is listed once per month inside
   it; a quarterly or yearly routine asked over a month is judged on the
   quarter or year that contains that month. Question: is one line per
   sub-period the wanted shape, or one line per policy?

2. **`routine` cuts at as-of.** The command accepts `--as-of` (default
   today) and sees nothing after it: a period that has not started yet is
   `missing` with the reason "period starts after <as-of>", and the
   current month checked early in the month is `missing` until the row
   arrives (silence is red). Exit 0 only when every line is `done`.

3. **DECISION-DUE is strictly `review_by < as_of`**, as ADR section 7
   says; a decision reviewed on its review date is `open` with "0 days
   left", due the day after. Closed decisions are never due.

4. **`closes` must name a kill policy** (E4): any other policy is a
   `BAD-CLOSES` problem and the decision is dropped, so it closes
   nothing; the loader keeps its two-argument signature and takes the
   kill ids as an optional third argument (`kill_ids`), which `cli` passes.

5. **A decision's `status` does not affect closing**: a decision with
   `closes: <kill id>` dated after the crossing closes the kill whether
   its own status is `open` or `closed`. Question: should only `closed`
   decisions count?

6. **Findings in `check`.** DECISION-DUE carries the decision's `policy`
   (None when the decision names none) and cites `decisions.yml:<line>`;
   it is red and blocks green. Row-level decisions.yml problems are red
   findings too (the file is still parsed; exit 1, not 3).

7. **Text shapes.** `routine`: `<done|missing> <id> <title> [<period>]`
   then citations, summary `ALL DONE|MISSING routine <period> as of
   <date>: n of m done`. `decisions`: due first, then open, then closed,
   each with `review by <date> (<n> days left|overdue)` or `decided
   <date> closes <id>`, summary `DUE|NONE DUE decisions as of <date>: ...`.
   Both JSON shapes carry `version`, `command`, `findings`, `files`,
   `problems`; `routine` adds `period`, `all_done`, `routines`; `decisions`
   adds `due`, `decisions` with `days_until_review` (null when closed).
