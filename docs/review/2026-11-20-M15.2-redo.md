## VERDICT: NEXT

**The REDO.** It does what Amendment C rules. Each pass asks the event 13 question first, and the first question's
  arguments are unchanged. The registry's bound is held:
- at a cut of the other answer;
- when the other read fails;
- while the root is not found.

The tests grow the fake channel between the two questions of one pass. F1 is closed wherever Sysmon logs a process's
  start before its value. That order is not measured, yet the threat model states it as fact.

**The Table's BV fix.** It joins all-zero ProcessGuids by pid in the follow and in the round, as ruled. But:
- the round says so only for process starts;
- three of the fix's costs are unsaid or misstated: a nested tree loses its mark, a connection loses its names, and
  the root's pid never leaves the tree's pid map.

All the must-fix items are sentences or one small condition the Table can make by hand.
