# M1.3 — questions and readings taken

1. **Where the draw lives.** No draw tables: the state of a draw is folded
   from its ledger entries (`server/draw.ts foldDraw`), so the server, a
   member's browser and an outside verifier compute it from the same
   evidence. The member set is recorded in `draw.opened` (active members at
   that moment, sorted); joins and leaves are refused while drawing.

2. **Closing commitments early.** "close-commits (organizer, or automatic
   at the deadline)". Reading taken (A3, nobody can bias the draw): the
   organizer may close early only when every member has committed;
   otherwise commitments close at the deadline. Likewise finalize before
   the reveal deadline only when every committer has revealed. An
   organizer can therefore not shut out a member by closing before they
   commit or finalizing before they reveal.

3. **"Automatic at the deadline on the next request" vs S2.3 (GET has no
   side effects).** Reading taken: `GET .../draw` reports the effective
   phase (commitments shown closed once the deadline passed) but writes
   nothing; the `draw.commits_closed` entry (`automatic: true`, actor
   `system`, listing who never committed) is written by the next POST to
   any draw route, in its own transaction, so it stands even when that
   request is then refused (a late commit gets 409 `commits_closed`).

4. **Reveals after the reveal deadline** are refused (409
   `reveals_closed`). Otherwise a member could wait, compute the order
   with and without their reveal, and pick; with the deadline, staying
   silent only ever places them last.

5. **Start date and the draw.** PATCH is draft-only, so a circle that
   opened its draw without a start date could never start. Reading taken:
   the draw needs a start date on or after the day of the reveal deadline
   (members commit knowing the schedule), and while drawing the organizer
   may change *only* the start date (one `circle.rule_changed` entry, as in
   draft); every other field is frozen once the draw opens. Found by the
   first full-draw test.

6. **A wrong secret** is 400 `wrong_secret` with an audit row
   (`draw.reveal_refused`) written even though the request fails; no
   ledger entry (nothing happened to the draw).

7. **Draw state for the client** (`GET /api/circles/:id/draw`): phase,
   deadlines, counts, my own commitment status; once finalized the whole
   record (members, every commitment and reveal with its ledger seq, seed,
   order, silent, never-committed) so the browser can recompute without
   trusting the server's order.

8. **Silent members in the ledger.** `draw.finalized` carries `silent`
   (never revealed, including never committed), `notCommitted`, the member
   set and the rule in words. A manual order set before the draw is
   cleared when it opens (`orderCleared: true` in `draw.opened`).
