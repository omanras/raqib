## 4. Deviations from ADR-0001

Readings where the implementation adds to or narrows the ADR text;
each is also in the question file named.

1. **Verdict `invalid` has a meaning** (ADR section 6 lists it without
   one): a policy touched by an invalid loader row; `unknown` is kept for
   undeclared shapes and unknowable values. (M1.1)
2. **Kill crossing is searched over the whole snapshot history** with a
   rolling window per snapshot date, not only inside the window ending
   at as-of, because "stays kill on every later date" needs the past.
   The `drawdown` measure of section 5 keeps the window-ending-at-as-of
   definition for cap/floor use. (M1.1)
3. **`income` and `expense` measures are spelled `sum: income` and
   `sum: expense`**; the ADR names the measures but gives no YAML. (M0.2)
4. **`{pct: P}` is accepted only on kill**, `{value}`, `{pct_of}`,
   `{months_of}` only on cap and floor; the ADR says `{pct}` is "for
   drawdown" without forbidding the rest. (M0.2)
5. **Exit 3 also covers a parsed but invalid policies.yml** (missing
   base currency, duplicate id, undeclared category in a measure); the
   ADR says "could not be read or parsed". (M1.1)
6. **Text output adds a `findings:` block and a GREEN/RED summary line**
   after the one-line-per-policy block of section 8; JSON adds
   `command`, `base_currency`, `files`, `problems`, `scope`, `declared`
   and per-policy `findings` to the shape of section 8. (M1.1, M1.2)
7. **`routine` accepts `--as-of`** and `review` accepts `--as-of` and
   `-o -`; section 8 lists neither. (M1.2, M2.1)
8. **The review's policy board has one column per month of the period**
   and the income-vs-expense chart shows the trailing twelve months;
   section 9 says "the policy board" and "by month". (M2.1)
9. **A policy's period for holdings-based cap/floor is the month of
   as-of** (shown as "as of <date>"); the ADR does not say. (M1.1)
10. **`snapshot_max_age_days`, `holdings.csv` and `decisions.yml` are
    required**; the ADR marks only `rates.csv` optional, which is
    followed, but a household without holdings must still ship the
    file and the field. (M0.2, M1.1)

Two **scope deviations**, both disclosed in their task's question file
and commit message: in M1.3 one token, and in M2.1 one obsolete test
function, were removed from `tests/test_cli.py`, a file outside those
tasks' scope. Both removed the builder's own forward-looking M1.2
assertion that `init` / `review` were "not available yet", which the
later tasks' exit criteria contradicted; the gate could not be green
otherwise, STOP is reserved for other cases, and the product was not
shaped to satisfy a stale test. The commander may revert either.

  with `PYTHONPATH`; `init` needs `examples/` next to the package.

  finding with the old value is a v0.2 candidate (FAILURE_MODEL).
- **Same-asset conflicts inside one holdings snapshot** are summed as
  two lots, not flagged (M0.1 item 8).
- **A kill history with one unvaluable snapshot** (no rate) is
  `unknown` for good until a rate is added (M1.1 item 2).
- The readings in every question file await the commander's answers;
  each can be reversed in a later HANDOFF without touching the others.
