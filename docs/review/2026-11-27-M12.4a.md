### E2

7. blocker. A refusal of 15 does not hold past one start. winservice.py:326-327 with 332-335.
      - A place the runner made or closed, then found not empty after the close, is refused with 15. It is left as the
     close made it: owner SY or BA, list CLOSED, and what was put in it still there.
      - At the next start (reboot, or the system starting it again) that place is "closed and owned right", so it is
     taken as it is. That road never reads the contents.
   - So Q3's claim and SERVICE.md:306-307 ("what a stranger left is not adopted") hold for one start only.
      - It was never measured whether an item put in during the window loses a stranger's access when the folder is
     closed. Amendment E measured items that took the new list. An item a stranger owns keeps its owner, and an owner
     can always change his item's list.
      - Fix: the Table rules what a place refused with 15 is at its next start (AE-2 rules only "taken as it is"). Add
     a test of two starts on one stand-in.
8. blocker. The place is checked and changed by name, and the reads follow links. winservice.py:208-216, 222, 229,
  260, 272, 336-337.
   - Each check and each change looks the place's name up again. Only os.lstat does not follow a link.
      - GetNamedSecurityInfoW, os.listdir, SetNamedSecurityInfoW and open() each resolve the name anew, and follow a
     link or junction.
      - Nothing ties the folder whose kind was read to the folder whose owner and list are read, which is closed, or
     which is written under. The final kind() at 336 is a separate lookup made before the final read at 337.
      - The exposed case is a folder a plain user made first, since he may remove and remake that name; AE-2 refuses
     this folder only by its owner. There the runner can:
          - (a) set the closed list on a folder that is not the place. That breaks AE-2's MUST NOT TOUCH, and the
       refusal with 11 at 336 comes only after the change.
          - (b) accept a closed, rightly owned folder, then write the text file and the watch's whole record under a
       name that resolves elsewhere.
   - The candidate never checked a link, so none of this was measured.
      - Fix: the Table rules how the checks and the use are tied to one object. This is a design question, and the
     calls it needs were not measured. Until then, SERVICE.md:299 and 309-310 and the THREAT_MODEL paragraph must not
     say the place is not followed or that it alone is touched. AE-5 should put a link and a junction at the place.
9. should fix. The link check is new, unmeasured and untested. winservice.py:209 and 214, SERVICE.md:299, and
  EXIT_WORDS[11] all say "any reparse point".
      - On Windows, os.lstat opens link and junction reparse points themselves and lets the system resolve other
     kinds. Whether FILE_ATTRIBUTE_REPARSE_POINT is then present is not known here.
   - No test calls the real System.kind. The stand-in returns "link" by fiat (tests/test_winservice.py:92-94).
   - A symlink can be made on the gate's Linux and a junction on its Windows job.
   - Fix: a real-object test of kind(), and the claim narrowed to what was measured or tested.
10. note. The owner check (winservice.py:245, 316-319) compares the exact text "SY" or "BA".
      - The SDDL converter writes these two as aliases, not as S-1-5-18 or S-1-5-32-544. A full-SID form would only
     refuse (13), so the comparison fails safe.
   - Not measured: the owner-alone text (3a), and which of SY or BA the system's account gives a folder it makes.
   - The E5 real test (tests:403-411) asserts only that the owner is non-empty and carries no "O:".
      - A list equal in meaning but written in another order or with other flags counts as open. An empty one is
     re-closed; a non-empty one is refused 14 with the words "is open" (329). That fails safe, but the words mislead.
11. note. `_drive_path` (289-290) is lexical. It refuses a relative path, `..` and a share. It accepts a drive letter
  that may be a network or substituted drive. It never checks ProgramData, or any parent, for a link or for its owner.
  This rests on the environment being the administrators' (Q8). The THREAT_MODEL paragraph should say so.
12. note. "Empty" is os.listdir alone (222, 328, 334). It does not see a stream on the folder itself. A handle opened
  while the folder was open keeps its granted access after the close, on both the made and the closed roads. AE-6
  names a file held open, not the folder. Not measured.

  GUIDE.md's row. Rule explicitly on the `service --help` change under E7.
