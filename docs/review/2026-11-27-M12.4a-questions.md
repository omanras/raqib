## Q1. RUNNING before the place, or START_PENDING until it is closed?

Chosen: the measured order. The service's main registers the handler and reports RUNNING at once (as the
candidate did, 0.14 to 0.16 s); the main thread then makes the place ready, begins the text file and starts the
watch. A refusal of the place is reported as STOPPED with its own number after RUNNING. Because a Stop may come
between RUNNING and the watch's start, the interrupt is raised only while the watch runs (under a lock); a Stop
before that means the watch is not begun (exit 0, said in the test of E4). The other road - START_PENDING with no
control accepted until the place is closed, then RUNNING - reports no RUNNING for a place that is refused, but it
was not measured (the candidate never reported START_PENDING). For the Table: keep the measured order, or measure
the other.

## Q2. The service-specific numbers

Chosen: one number for each refusal of the place, not one for "the place" as a whole, so that the system's log
says which: 10 no ProgramData, 11 a link, 12 not a folder, 13 the owner, 14 open and not empty, 15 not closed
(not read, made or closed; read back other; not empty after closing), 16 the text file; and 20 the watch refused
(its result 2), 21 the watch ended without being asked (or with no result of its own), 22 an exception out of the
watch. They are constants in raqib/winservice.py (EXIT_*, EXIT_WORDS) and the table in docs/specs/SERVICE.md is
held to them by a test. The system is told ERROR_SERVICE_SPECIFIC_ERROR (1066) with the number: not measured (the
candidate always stopped with 0); AE-5's run should read the number back from the system (`sc query` gives
SERVICE_EXIT_CODE). The process's own exit is 0 where the system was told 0, else 2 (Raqib's "refused"); a
service's process exit is not what the system shows, so the numbers were not made exit codes of the process.

## Q3. The window between making the place and closing it

AE-2 says absent: made, closed, read back. The runner makes the folder with os.mkdir and then sets the closed
list; in between, the folder has what ProgramData gives (every user may create in it), so a plain user could put
something in it then. Chosen: a folder the runner made or closed that is not empty after it was closed is refused
(15), so nothing a stranger put in is adopted. Making the folder with the closed list at once (CreateDirectoryW
with a SECURITY_ATTRIBUTES holding the descriptor) would leave no window, but it was not measured, so it was not
built. For the Table: measure it in AE-5 or a later task, or keep the check after closing.

## Q4. The text file: its names and its bound

Chosen: `service.txt` in the place, the one before kept once as `service.before.txt`; at most 8 MiB (TEXT_MAX,
8388608 bytes, its last line included). The bound counts bytes as written (UTF-8, what cannot be encoded escaped).
A watch that runs for weeks fills it and then writes nothing more there until its next start (its last line says
so; the record goes on). For the Table: is 8 MiB right, and is a turn of the file within one run (not asked by
AE-3) wanted later?

## Q5. The waits

CONNECT_WAIT 25 s (the dispatcher must call the service's main or fail; else exit 2, said) and END_WAIT 15 s
(waited for the dispatcher's return after STOPPED) are the candidate's. STOP_PENDING's wait hint is 30 s, the
candidate's. None of these is the wait of M12.4b (a watch started while the one before left its state unfinished).

## Q6. The service's name

The table names the service `RaqibWatch` (SERVICE_NAME). A service of its own process may be installed under any
name; the system does not check the table's. M12.4c chooses the installed name; if it differs, SERVICE_NAME
should follow it so the two read the same.

## Q8. Where ProgramData comes from

Chosen: the environment the service manager gives the process (`ProgramData`), as AE-2 says ("the folder the
system names ProgramData to its services"). Accepted only as a whole path of a drive (a letter, a colon and a
separator; no `..`); a share (`\\host\share`) is refused, as no capture may rest on the network. The system's
environment is the administrators' to set, and an administrator is not in the threat model's reach. The folder
the system's known-folder call gives (SHGetKnownFolderPath) was not used: not measured.

## Q9. The watch's result 2

A watch that refuses to start returns 2, which is exit 20 to the system whether a Stop came or not. Today that is
also what the measured "another raqib watch --host is watching this home" refusal comes to; how the service waits
then is M12.4b's, and nothing of it was built here.

## REDO

The REDO of M12.4a (ADR-0013 Amendment F, AF-1 to AF-4), by a second builder on c125f62's branch. Each question
says what was chosen meanwhile and why. None is answered locally. Nothing above this heading was changed.

### Existing tests changed

Only tests/test_winservice.py, which is in the task's scope, and only where it held what this task changes: the
closed list as one constant (now two, AF-1), the exits table in decimal alone (now n, decimal and hexadecimal,
AF-3), the road that closed an open empty place and the number 15 (gone, AF-1), and the statuses that began with
RUNNING (now START_PENDING, AF-2). Every other assertion of it was kept or made stricter; the source test now also
refuses a call that sets an owner or a list, `listdir`, `lstat` and the name of 15. No other test of tests/ was
changed: raqib/winservice.py starts no program, so tests/test_own_env.py's seven starts and
tests/test_own_tree.py's lists hold as they were.

### R1. A number for a place that could not be made or read

AF-1 numbers 11 to 14 and removes 15; it does not number the roads where the system's calls themselves fail: the
making call answers an error other than 183, the list's text is not taken, or the one handle cannot be opened or
read (an absent name, error 2, after "it already exists"; the attributes or the owner and list not read). Chosen:
one new number, 17 ("the place could not be made, or could not be read through one handle on its name"). 15 is not
used again, so that a 15 in a log of c125f62 is never read as something else. For the Table: 17, or another.

### R2. A number for the runner's own exception

AF-4 asks for a number of its own and names none. Chosen: 23 ("an exception of the runner's own (not the
watch's)"), next to 22 (the watch's). It is also what STOPPED carries until the road decides the exit, so that a
run that ends before any decision is never told 0. Its trace is written in the text file where one is begun; before
that, its words go to the error stream (lost in a service), and the number is the only trace.

### R3. What the system is told when the text file was ended by a failed write

AF-4 rules that the runner keeps that it happened, and does not say what it does with it. Chosen: where the system
would be told exit 0, it is told 18 ("a write to the text file failed and ended it (the watch's record went on)");
a number of the watch's own (20 to 23) is kept as it is, since it says why the watch ended. Why: the text file is
the one place a person reads the service's words, and once a write to it failed the exit is the only place left to
say so (silence is an alarm); AE-4's exit 0 is about the watch's result, and this is the runner's, not the
watch's. The other road: exit 0, and the fact kept only in the runner. For the Table to rule.

### R4. The text the place is made with

The making call is given `D:PAI(A;OICI;FA;;;SY)(A;OICI;FA;;;BA)`, the text the candidate gave it in Amendment F's
measurements, where the folder read back `D:P(A;OICI;FA;;;SY)(A;OICI;FA;;;BA)`. Giving it `D:P...` instead was not
measured, so it was not chosen. Both forms are taken at the read (AF-1).

### R5. START_PENDING's hint, and a Stop before RUNNING

START_PENDING is told with a hint of 5000 ms (the candidate's, in Amendment F's measurements) and accepts no
control, as the candidate did. A Stop that reaches the handler before RUNNING all the same (a stand-in only; the
system sends none to a service that accepts none) is obeyed: STOP_PENDING, no RUNNING, the watch not begun, exit
0 and its one line. For the Table: keep, or refuse such a Stop.

### R6. The owner and the list read as one text

The one handle's owner and list are written as one text (the owner's and the list's information together, as the
candidate's read did) and parted at its `D:`. An owner the converter writes as a full SID rather than SY or BA is
refused (13): it fails safe. That a folder made by the system's own account reads back BA (and not SY) is Amendment
F's measurement; both are taken.

### R7. A watch that returned no result after a Stop

AF-4 rules exit 0 for a Stop that came before the watch gave any result. Chosen: that is the watch not begun, or
ended by its interrupt (KeyboardInterrupt) with no result. A watch whose call returned None after a Stop is still
21 (it returned, with no result of its own), as before. For the Table: keep that line, or tell 0 for it too.

### R8. An interrupt that surfaces outside the watch's call

At most one interrupt is raised, and only while the watch's call runs (under the lock that also clears it). Where
it surfaces after the call returned (a Stop as the watch ended), it is caught: inside the watch's road it changes
nothing and the watch's result is kept; anywhere else in the runner it is caught, said in the text file, and the
exit is decided from the watch's outcome kept. The window and where Python lands the interrupt in it were not
measured on Windows; the tests raise it by a stand-in at a call outside the watch's.

### R9. A junction in the real-object test

E5 asks for a junction or a link where the test can make one. Python's standard library has no public call that
makes a junction; the test uses `_winapi.CreateJunction` (CPython's own, used by its own tests) under the test's
temp folder, and skips that part where it is missing or refused, as it does for `os.symlink`. For the Table: keep,
or a link alone.

### R10. The standard input

Only the standard output and error streams are replaced; the standard input stays None, as it was in c125f62,
which ran so in Amendment F's field run (the watch ran and stopped with its final report). The docs now say so.
