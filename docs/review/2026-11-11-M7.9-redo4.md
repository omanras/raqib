## Findings

**A1 (must-fix; AB-4 MUST NOT TOUCH; a wrong knock; A8)** discover.py:2698-2713, host.py:1884-1889, THREAT_MODEL.md:549-550.
- **The cause:** `_footprints` keeps ONE link per footprint. Pass 3 walks candidates in pid order (discover.py:2159), and the first match wins.
- **Inputs:**
  - Footprint `C:\Users\user\.kimi`; rounds 1-2 dark (the agent is idle).
  - Round 3: K (powershell pid 5888, unknown, talking at the sample) gets a sampled child kimi.exe. K links in pass 3 (`_named`).
  - Also in round 3: U (pid 5000, unknown, by the channel) whose child's Sysmon 1 CommandLine holds `C:\Users\user\.kimi\config.toml` (the AB-4 test's own fixture shape).
- **What happens:** U comes first, so the link is U. The host forces the footprint dark: streak 3, was_live false, so AGENT-DARK-LIVE fires in round 3 while K, knocked at a sample, links the footprint.
- **Why it is wrong:** before M7.9, U was unseen, K linked the footprint, it was live, and nothing knocked. THREAT_MODEL's "a footprint it alone links" is untrue here. The test (:974-985) has one linking tree only.

**A2 (must-fix; A8, AB-2's "the words do not lie")** README.md:200 "exit 0 nothing new"; cli.py:115-116 "Exit 0 trees listed and nothing new".
- **Inputs:** a watch with M = 2 updater trees listed by the channel.
- **What happens:** it exits 0, while the help and the README table tell the person that exit 0 means nothing new.
- **Why it is wrong:** AB-2 replaced exactly these words ("nothing new") in RESULT at M > 0 because they are untrue.

**A3 (should-fix; the Table's, Q-19; AA-1 case 4; YAML:27, THREAT_MODEL:551)** host.py:1772, 1810, 1821, 2599-2608.
- **Inputs:** an unknown process P is older than the watch (its CreationDate was read). It is idle at round 1's sample, but talks and starts a child between the reader's making and round 1's read; all its events are after `since`.
- **What happens:**
  - In round 1, P is a candidate by the channel, so it is history, and history sticks.
  - Never tagged or counted.
  - In round 3, P is a candidate at a sample and is not knocked (`not history` is false).
- **Why it matters:** before M7.9 it was no tree in round 1 and was knocked in round 3. The window widens round 1's span: round 1 waits for the look, app.py:1330-1333.
- **What a ruling changes:** "history" as built keeps tag = count, but the YAML hole and THREAT_MODEL must state this exception. "Not history when the candidacy rests on events after the start" would tag and count it, and knock it later.
