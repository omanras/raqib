MUST-FIX

R-1 (SILENCE) A quiet or kept tree's beats are put into an entry outside the window, so they are judged nowhere and
  counted nowhere.
- Where: raqib/host.py:2626-2631 (`_rhythm` appends the entry with from=round_from, then `_bin`), host.py:1317-1327
  (`_bin`), host.py:1274 (`rhythm_of` keeps only rounds > n-R).
- What I read: a source's beats are read one round late. E1's own test shows it: cycle_rounds == [1,3,4] at
  tests/test_host_quiet_rhythm.py:113. So a quiet line in round N always carries beats whose times fall before its own
  entry's `from`. `_bin` therefore puts them into the tree's previous ring entry, whatever its age. `del ring[:-r]`
  trims by entries, not rounds, so an old entry stays as a sink.
- Case (K=3, W=30, R=10):
  - The tree is a candidate in round 1 (entry e1), then quiet with no beats in rounds 2-11.
  - It makes one cycle in each of intervals 11, 12 and 13; these are read in rounds 12, 13 and 14.
  - Round 12: the ring is [e1, e12]. The beats go to e1, which rhythm_of drops (1 <= 12-10), so cycles = 0. Round 13:
    they go to e12, cycles = 1. Round 14: they go to e13, cycles = 2. No finding.
  - The same beats on a tree that is a candidate every round: e11, e12, e13, cycles = 3, it fires in round 14.
- Result: a burst of exactly K cycles after about R-1 quiet rounds never fires on a quiet tree. The first interval's
  beats were consumed by `_source_beats`, so they are no longer counted by `_late_beats` either (host.py:2688-2700).
  Before this commit they were "said" in RHYTHM BEATS; now they are said nowhere.
- Likelihood: high for an agent that idles for R rounds and then works (the commander's long-lived tree).
- What is missing: the hole at HOST-RHYTHM.yaml:68, THREAT_MODEL.md:588-590 and the AQ-4 test
  (tests/test_host_quiet_rhythm.py:254-262) state only the merge case. None of them states the loss, and no test
  covers it.
