## Findings

**C1 (must-fix: AD-1's property not met; AD-2 touches rounds after the first; a NEW-AGENT that came before M7.9 is silenced, and an AGENT-DARK-LIVE knock appears that did not)**
- **Where:**
  - host.py:1780-1781: AD-2 puts history on the guid tree's key.
  - host.py:2598-2606: `_same_tree` gives a later sampled tree that earlier key. This happens when the sampled tree has no guid, its start is read, and no event of the guid is older than that start. Two sampled-only trees are skipped (2582).
  - host.py:1779: history sticks to the key.
  - host.py:1906: the footprint's live reads `self.trees[lk]["history"]`, the history of the channel watch's tree.
- **Inputs (a):**
  - look_watch (the watch starts at 07:59:30).
  - Round 1: `talking_unread(..., image=KIMI)` plus footprints.txt `C:\Users\user\.kimi`. kimi.exe pid 5888 talks and has a child; its CreationDate is blank. Round 1's channel is :1697's read (`shell_events(rec=500, create=False, at=08:00:00)` with PS replaced by KIMI).
  - Round 2: the same sample with CreationDate `2026-11-10T07:50:00.000Z`; empty channel.
  - Rounds 3-5: no pid 5888; the footprint is listed.
- **What happens:**
  - Round 1 is as in :1688: two trees, both history. K_G = (5888, kimi, "guid {..}") is kept in guid_keys.
  - Round 2: `_same_tree` maps the sampled kimi onto K_G. The tree is history and was a candidate in round 1, so no NEW-AGENT comes. The footprint is linked by it, but live_key is not set, so was_live is False.
  - Rounds 3-5: dark, and AGENT-DARK-LIVE knocks at round 5.
- **Without the channel (the replay) and before M7.9:**
  - Round 2's key is new (no tree has a guid), so it is not history: NEW-AGENT (high) in round 2.
  - The footprint is live from round 2.
  - Rounds 3-5 give "dark after a live tree (note)", no knock.
- **Inputs (b):** powershell, with round 1's channel holding an event 1 of pid 5888 at 08:00:05 (the same image reusing the pid after the sample), and round 2 sampling pid 5888 with CreationDate 08:00:05.
  - The starts match (2584-2587), so the round-2 tree is history.

It counts as kept when connections are unread and its root is sampled (or there is no sample), and as quiet when its root is sampled. A brief tree gets neither.

  - It is incomplete (C4), and its claim about history is false (C1).
