## Q-1 (AM-4, F3) "beats after the last round" is not explained by the times

The test `tests/test_event_logged_time.py::test_e4_am4_rhythm_beats_of_the_made_up_export_before_and_after`
states RHYTHM BEATS for a made-up export of the field's shape (two shells'
starts and a file at the watch's minute; their two talks stamped 26 days 7
hours behind the time they were logged), read by a tree's os follow:

| the follow's lines read in             | before AM-1 | after AM-1 |
|----------------------------------------|-------------|------------|
| the last round (attach in round 2 of 2)| 2           | 4          |
| a round with two rounds after it (of 4)| 0           | 0          |

Before AM-1 the two talks are before the watch's start: the bounded follow
writes no line for them, so only the two starts are beats; after AM-1 the
talks are live, and are beats too. Whether the beats are "after the last
round" is decided by the round that read the follow's lines, not by the
times: a source's beats are taken one round late (M7.5 REDO N-4, Q-5), so
lines read by the last round are in no round, and HOST-RHYTHM never judges
them. That fits the field (the Kimi tree's acts two minutes before the last
round of a watch whose rounds are minutes apart), but the builder did not
change it (AM-4: "changes nothing there").

Question: should the final poll's source beats be judged by one last
HOST-RHYTHM pass at the watch's end (so a tree's acts in its last round are
judged), or stay counted and listed as now ("beats after the last round: N,
not judged by HOST-RHYTHM")?

## Q-2 (AM-1) a process start's time where its two times disagree

AM-1 applies to every Sysmon event, event 1 included: where an event 1's
UtcTime is more than 60 s from its logged time, its record's time (and so
its match with a sampled CreationDate, within 2 s) is the logged time. The
field's event 1 agreed, so nothing observed moves; where the two agree the
match is byte for byte as before (tested). Is that the Table's reading, or
should event 1 keep its UtcTime for that match whatever the log says?
