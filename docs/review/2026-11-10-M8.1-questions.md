describe (rail, verdict header with one primary action, cards, the round
strip, switches and pills, sharp text on Windows), over the same Controller,
Session, HostSession and RulesPanel; no behaviour change.

## What was built (E1-E7)
- E1 `THEME` (raqib/app.py): ADR-0009's tokens for light and dark (ground,
  card, ink, muted, the rail #141C2B in both, the accent #2B59D9 / #7AA2FF),
  the levels by the exit-code scheme, a pill ground per level, the levels on
  the rail, `pairs` (every text / ground pair the window draws) and
  `theme_pairs(mode)`; no starting token had to change - every pair is at
  least 4.5:1 in both modes (the dark selection was set at #1E2D50 so the
  red row stays 4.9:1). Fonts by `pick_font` from ADR-0009's families (ui,
  display, mono) at four sizes (15, 11, 10, 9 pt). THEME stays the only
  place a colour lives (the AST tests hold).
- E2 the rail (name and its mark - four rounds, the last not read; what is
  watched in words; Host / Project / Rules / Log; "N rules off" in the rail's
  amber, red while suppressed > 0; Open page; the doctrine sentence) and on
  every screen the verdict header: the level's pill with its word, one
  sentence (`verdict_sentence`: "HOST: 2 TREE(S)" -> "2 trees on this host",
  "DOCTOR: 1 MISSING, 0 UNSURE" -> "Checks: 1 missing, 0 unsure"), beside it
  `verdict_note` ("Not a clearance.", after the suppressed count when there
  is one), the detail, and the screen's one filled button (Watch this host /
  Stop host watch, Start watching, Stop / Back, Add rule file..., Jump to
  newest). Log is a screen of its own (the host watch's or the project
  watch's lines, followed at the end). The first-run lines and the doctrine
  stay; "Not a clearance." stands in every green state (C-1..C-3 tests hold).
- E3 `strip_cells(state, fit)` and `strip_text(cells, total)`, pure, from
  the host state's `rounds_tail` (the 50 round lines host.py already keeps),
  its findings and reader holes by round, `rounds` and `reader`: clean /
  finding / unread (not read, partly read or late) / before; newest at the
  right; as many as fit after the sentence has its width; a round without a
  line is drawn amber, never skipped (A3). Drawn on a Canvas. host.py was
  not touched: the state already carried what the strip needs.
- E4 ttk styles on the clam theme everywhere (the "vista" theme of M7.6b is
  gone: it keeps grey OS buttons): one filled primary, flat secondary
  buttons, rounded fields, tables with tall rows, no grid lines, a quiet
  header and a tinted selection (sort kept, the arrow drawn), thin
  scrollbars that hide when nothing scrolls. The Switch, the Pill and the
  disclosure are drawn by the window: anti-aliased pictures painted from
  THEME's colours (`paint`, `box`, `switch_shape`, `chevron_shapes`, pure)
  used as ttk image elements, each with a focus picture (a ring in the
  accent). Tab reaches each control; Space acts (ttk), Enter acts the same
  (`bind_class` on TButton and TCheckbutton). Doctor's checks are rows (pill,
  name, sentence on tab stops). "Could not see (N)" lists every hole of the
  screen with the notes under them (replaces C-5's footer); "Checks", "Watch
  options" (closed by default, as the HANDOFF says) and "What the overlay
  applies" are disclosures too.
- E5 the Rules screen: a row per rule - its Switch (RulesPanel.set), name,
  the YAML's title, class and budget, pills for kind, severity and origin,
  "off by the overlay" / "off in its YAML" / "thresholds set", each editable
  threshold as a stepper within its YAML bounds (RulesPanel.threshold; the
  measurement's words beside it: bounds, the measured range, the YAML's
  value), the fixed ones in words, "Reset to the YAML" when there is
  something to undo (RulesPanel.reset); a filter box; Add rule file...; the
  list scrolls (wheel, and to the row that takes the keyboard's focus).
- E6 `declare_dpi_awareness()` in run() before `tk.Tk()` (Windows only:
  shcore.SetProcessDpiAwareness(1), system awareness, as IDLE does);
  `dark_title_bar(hwnd, mode)` from the Window in dark mode (Windows only:
  dwmapi.DwmSetWindowAttribute(own frame, 20, then 19)). Both through
  `_win_api()`, the only place app.py imports ctypes; a failure of either is

  opens as before. `scaled(n, scale)` is the one scale function,
  `screen_scale(dpi)` reads Tk's dpi (1.0 at 96, 1.25 at 120, 1.5 at 144);
  minsize 900x600 at 1.0.
- E7 tests/test_app_design.py (new) and the changes below; README's window
  section rewritten for what a person sees.

## Questions (built meanwhile as said; the Table rules)
- Q-1 RulesPanel gains one read-only attribute, `titles` (rule id -> the
  YAML's title), filled where it already loads the rules: E5 asks for the
  title and the listing (overlay.py, out of Scope) does not carry it.
  Nothing it does changed. Accept, or move the title into overlay.listing
  in a later task?
- Q-2 The Log screen's "one primary action" is "Jump to newest" (the text
  also follows its end by itself while it is there). Nothing else on that
  screen is an action. Accept?
- Q-3 Pills are display only (a level, a kind): Tab does not stop on them;
  every pill that acts would be a button. E4's "Tab reaches each" is read as
  "each control that acts" (the Switch, the disclosure, the steppers, the
  buttons, the rail). Accept?
- Q-4 One disclosure open at a time (opening one closes the other), and,
  while one is open, the first-run lines fold and the trees table asks for
  two rows instead of three: so every screen with any disclosure open asks
  for no more than 900x600 and the table keeps the stretch. The first-run
  lines also fold while the host is watched (they say what the window shows
  before the first look). Accept?
- Q-5 Doctor's notes are grey "note" pills now (they were amber lines tagged
  incomplete); every level's colour has its word, and amber is the hole's.
  Accept?
- Q-6 The status pills (the badges, C-2) show the level's word in bold and
  the badge's text ("alarm 2 holes (see the alarms)"), not "text [alarm]".
  `badge_items` returns (word, text, level). Accept?
- Q-7 The anti-aliased edges of the drawn controls are blends of two THEME
  colours computed for the pictures (never passed to a colour option; the
  AST tests hold). Accept that as "THEME is the only place a colour lives"?
- Q-8 The verdict's sentences are the window's own words (SENTENCES in
  app.py); the state words of ui.status, the page, the report and the
  terminal are unchanged. The checks title (`checks_state`) says "1
  missing" / "1 unsure" in sentence case; its second value is the title's
  level now, not "open?" (the Table's note: closed until clicked).
- Q-9 Not verified here (Windows only): the DPI awareness on a scaled
  display, the dark title bar, Segoe UI Variable's metrics at 900x600. The
  CI windows jobs run every Tk test, the 900x600 fit tests among them (each
  screen and each disclosure open); the dark-mode tests there call the real
  DwmSetWindowAttribute on the test's own window only.
