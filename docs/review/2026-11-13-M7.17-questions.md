## Q1 (scope) - HOST-RHYTHM's fixtures are not made by make.py alone

E3 asks for one quiet positive and two quiet negatives "made by
tests/fixtures/host/make.py", listed under HOST-RHYTHM.yaml's fixtures.
In the code, HOST-RHYTHM is a GENERATED rule (raqib/rules/fixturegen.py:87,
`GENERATED = ("HOST-RHYTHM", "AGENT-DARK-LIVE")`): its scenarios are planned
by `fixturegen.plan` (the RHYTHM branch: `rhythm_scenarios`, `os_scenarios`,
`os_rounds`, `w_scenarios`, `r_scenarios`) and its synthetic rounds are
built by `fixturegen.synthetic_inventory`. make.py only imports those.

`fixturegen.generate` walks the YAML's own fixture list and raises for a
fixture it has no plan for (fixturegen.py, in `generate`:
`raise GenerateError("%s: no generator for fixture %s" ...)`). It is called:
- by the thresholds' measure, raqib/overlay.py `measure` (`made =
  fixturegen.generate(rule, builder)`), so a person could no longer set
  HOST-RHYTHM's k, w or r (every value would be refused);
- by tests/test_rules.py `test_a1_rules_never_reach_a_model_or_the_network`
  (`fixturegen.generate(r, stub, rules)` for every GENERATED rule), and by
  tests/test_thresholds.py through `fixturegen.generate(rule,
  host.fixture_builder())`.

So a quiet fixture listed in HOST-RHYTHM.yaml without a plan for it in
raqib/rules/fixturegen.py breaks the measure and the gate; a quiet fixture
not listed is not the rule's fixture and is not in its measured budget
(E3, AP-3). raqib/rules/fixturegen.py is not under Scope. Asked: add
raqib/rules/fixturegen.py to M7.17's Scope (a quiet round name in
`synthetic_inventory`, three scenarios and their os rounds in the RHYTHM
branch of `plan`), or say where the quiet fixtures are to be planned.

What the builder read, for the Table's next HANDOFF (not built):
- a quiet round can be a synthetic round whose inventory holds no candidate
  and no act-root and a `processes` list with the rhythm tree's root (pid,
  image, started): `rhythm_inventory` writes no `processes`, so today no
  synthetic tree can be quiet (with `processes` absent, `alive` is None and

- `host.fixture_rounds` appends `fixturegen.os_lines(k, name.split(":")[1],

- with the tree a candidate in round 1, quiet from round 2, os lines before

  positive. The negatives: the same with talks only (no cycle); and a few
  beats (K-1 cycles: os lines before rounds 2 and 3, four rounds), so the
  last round still has a quiet line and does not fire.

## Q2 - a tree kept by a blind round (connections unread)

`_apply` has three kinds of tree absent from the round: quiet (root in the
sample), gone (root not in it) and kept (the round's connections unread:
`kept`, "not judged"). AP-2 names the quiet. A kept tree's source beats wait
too, until the tree is in a round or the final poll. Asked: is a kept tree
also given its line (AP-1 says "whether or not that round's sample sees its
tree talk"), or does it stay "not judged" and its beats wait?

## Q3 - the connection state of a quiet line (asked, so that nothing is decided locally)

`_rhythm` takes talks through `talk_beats`, which calls `_talks(key, t)`;
`_talks` resets the tree's open connections to those of `t`. A quiet line
built from a `t` with no connections would reset that state, and when the
tree is in a round again its connection to the same remote counts as a new
talk (where no bytes are read: Windows) where today it does not. The
builder's reading: a quiet line takes only its sources' beats (no host
children, no host talks) and does not touch the connection state, so a
candidate's line gives what it gave. Asked to confirm.

## Note (not a question) - where a quiet tree's beats are binned

A ring entry is made only for a round the tree has a line in. A beat goes to
the latest entry whose interval began before it, so where a quiet round had
no line (no beat), a later beat of that interval lands in an older entry;
two cycles may then share one entry and count once (the rule's hole "several
cycles inside one round's interval count as one" covers it). The finding is
in the round after the poll that read the third cycle (a source's beats are
counted one round late).
