# M14.1c - questions and choices (ADR-0007 Amendment BY)

The builder's choices where the HANDOFF was silent, the places discover asks
whether a record is the channel's, and every test of tests/ that was followed
because it held, word for word or by count, a line, a list or a number this
task changes. None of these is answered here; each is for the Table.

## Where discover asks whether a record is the channel's

1. `_guid_instances` (Windows' ProcessGuid keying). Not used for the reader:
   it runs only in a Windows channel round (`channel=True`) and keys Sysmon
   records alone. The reader's records are keyed by a new `_reader_instances`
   (after `_instances` and `_guid_instances`, before the anchor): one process
   with the sampled process of its pid where the start ticks are the same;
   where the reader's ticks were not read, where the time its fork was heard
   and the sample's shown start (`started_at`) are within GUID_SAME_SECONDS,
   or the sample's start was not read; otherwise keyed apart
   (pid + REUSE * n). A child the reader gave is linked to the instance of
   its parent pid alive at its fork where the reader gave that instance.
   Q1: is the join by the fork's time (no ticks) right, and the fallback
   "the sample's start unread -> one by pid"?
2. `_channel_fields` (guid, brief, channel_started). Not applied on Linux:
   a Linux tree's root is a talker of the sample (a connection record comes
   from the sample alone), so a tree's root is sampled. The one exception is
   the ss fallback, where a talker's pid comes from ss's text and may be held
   by the reader alone; such a root is not marked `brief`. Q2: should it be?
3. `_without_channel`. Now also leaves out the reader's records (kind
   `procevents`), and is run where a reader's text was read: the round
   built without them gives `bare_class`, `candidate_by` ("sample" or
   "channel", the value the host's AA-1 path reads) and the footprints
   (AD-1). Q3: the word printed on Linux is the host's `by the channel`
   (the reader is the round's channel, BU-6); is that the word wanted?
4. `build`, the window-gap place (`s["kind"] == "sysmon" and channel`).
   Unchanged. A reader's text with no record would be "source procevents was
   read and holds no record"; the watch never gives an empty text (see C1).
5. `run`, the parser choice. `PARSERS["procevents"]` is `parse_procevents`;
   there is no keyed variant, and `channel` stays False on Linux.
6. `_starts` / `_link_verdict` (the channel's own start read in a channel
   round). The reader's records carry the sample's own key ("boot+N") as
   their `started`, so a link to or from a reader's process is dated as a
   sampled one's, with no channel. A record with no ticks has no key, so its
   link is "kept with a start unread" and BE-3's hole names it.
7. `_holes`' Windows line (`kinds & {..., "sysmon"}`). Unchanged.

## Other choices

- C1 (E1 against BY-2's MUST NOT TOUCH). The watch gives the `procevents`
  text only where the helper gave at least one process; a round whose helper
  gave nothing has no such text and is the round it was, byte for byte (a
  text with no line would add a source and a hole).
- C2. A process whose fork was not heard (`began` null: older than the
  reader, or its fork lost) is not given: E1's line needs `began`.
- C3 (BY-6, the watch's own). The reader's record of a process the watch
  started is left out of the text by the watch (`host.reader_text`): its pid
  in the watch's record (a copy taken before the round's sample, since the
  sample forgets an ended pid, and one after it), its parent the watch, its
  program's basename the recorded image, its fork within the record's window
  (own.alive's proof). E1's line may hold no other field, so the round cannot
  be told "this one is the watch's" in the text. They are not counted in the
  section. Q4: should they be counted, or given and taken into the watch's
  tree by discover instead (that needs a field or a head line E1 forbids)?
- C4. `run_ss` starts ss by `subprocess.Popen` inside `own.started`, and on
  its timeout ends that ss and waits, as `subprocess.run` did.
- C5. discover's inventory gains `from_reader` {given, joined, unread,
  by_fork, apart} only where a reader's text was read; the host sums given,
  joined, unread and by_fork for the section. `by_fork` (BY-1: "that is
  said") is printed as its own line only where it is not 0.
- C6 (BY-5). "After at least one answered ask" counts the round's own answer
  (an answer that carries a loss, a bound passed or its reader's end is a
  hole of the result). A loss or a bound passed is an episode while each
  answer shows its count grew. A refusal after a restart that answered before
  is a hole of the result too. Each hole of the result is also printed as a
  `HOLE round N: the process events: ...` line, and in the section with
  "(a hole of the result)".
- C7. A HostChild line of a reader's child carries no new field: its cites
  (src `procevents`) say it came from the reader (BY-4). Q5: is a field on
  the line wanted?
- C8 (E5). The Helper's input is made non-blocking at its start and the ask
  written by the pipe's own raw write (raqib/procevents.py may not call
  os.write: tests/test_procevents.py); a write that writes none or a part is
  `ended`, said as "its input was full: the ask could not be written without
  waiting". `procevents.command` lost its `isolated` parameter.
- C9 (E7). The stand-in runs wget only where `shutil.which("wget")` finds
  it, else says "wget not installed". X36 is n/a also where the stand-in did
  not run wget (no `start wget` line). X35 (BX-5, "X35 and the reader's
  bounds") is now MISSED unless the section says "past the reader's bounds:
  0".
- C10 (E2's byte test). The inventories of the three Linux fixtures of
  tests/, built by the code of 44770c1 (live and not), are kept under
  tests/fixtures/procevents-round/before/ with relative paths and the
  catalog's path taken out; the test compares this code's bytes to them.
- C11. E5's test uses the real ss where it is installed and a stand-in
  program elsewhere (the builder's sandbox has none); the reader's record of
  it is written by the test, not heard from a real reader.
