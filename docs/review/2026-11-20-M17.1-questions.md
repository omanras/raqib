## 1. GUIDE.md's new sentences need tests/test_readme_top.py's registry, which is not in Scope (the reason)

E5 asks GUIDE.md to say where the number is read, what a move of it means, where the changelog is and the update
steps for Windows and Linux. Every sentence of GUIDE.md is held by

    tests/test_readme_top.py::test_e3_every_sentence_of_the_old_readme_is_in_the_readme_or_the_guide_word_for_word

whose "New -> old" half (line 733) asserts

    assert added == [] and used == set(GUIDE_NEW) | set(CHANGED.values())

that is: every sentence, table row and code line of the guide is the old README's (the fixture
tests/fixtures/docs/README-before-M9.2.md) under the same heading, or is registered in `GUIDE_NEW` (the list
begun at line 214 and grown by later tasks at lines 240, 265, 294 and 298). A new sentence
in the guide fails it unless it is added to `GUIDE_NEW`.

Measured, not guessed: with one paragraph of four sentences added to GUIDE.md under "Install and watch" (and the
README's one sentence under "More"), that test failed with
`('Install and watch', 'Which Raqib runs is said by its number:')` the first of three items in `added`; the 25
other tests of tests/test_readme_top.py and tests/test_docs.py passed (the README sentence breaks nothing).

Scope names tests/ only "where it holds the number 0.1.0 as a literal, or holds word for word a line this task
changes". `GUIDE_NEW` holds neither: it is the registry of the guide's new lines, and adding to it is what
M12.1p's HANDOFF named in its own words ("tests/test_readme_top.py (only the guide's new or changed lines in its
registry)"). The same guard stopped M12.1 (95cd5de). So the builder did not edit it.

What would land it, none chosen: Scope gains "tests/test_readme_top.py (only: the guide's new lines appended to
`GUIDE_NEW`, as M12.1p's)". No other way was found: a table row, a code line or a heading of the guide is held
the same way, and a new heading of the guide would also need a link in README.md's "More" (line 606 of the test).
