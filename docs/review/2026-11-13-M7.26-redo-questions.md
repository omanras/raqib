calls `hostwin.self_tree(procs, 14)` with no starts and pins

test_a7) the walk is by images only, as before, and the docstring says so.

given starts, or is the image-only walk without dates to stay?

## Q3 (BF-3) - "under the reader's own pid" read as under the reader's tree

The unread hole names every process whose parent pid is in the reader's
proven tree (the reader, its launchers, and the helpers proven under them)
by a link not dated: "(the reader's own pid)" where the parent is the
reader or a launcher, "(in the reader's tree)" where it is a proven
helper. A process under a helper by a link not dated is also not taken for
the reader's (BE-2), so it is named too. Asked: confirm, or name only the
processes directly under the reader's and its launchers' pids.
