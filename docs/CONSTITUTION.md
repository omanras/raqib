# Qayd Constitution (public doctrine)

Qayd keeps the books and reads the policies. It does not advise, and it
does not touch the money.

## Articles
- **A1 Deterministic.** Every verdict comes from a declared policy applied
  to recorded rows. Same files, same verdict, on every machine. No model,
  no score, no "likely".
- **A2 Policies are declared, not inferred.** A limit, a floor, a routine, a
  red line, a kill condition: each is a line in policies.yml with its
  period, its measure and its threshold. Qayd never invents a policy from
  the data.
- **A3 Silence is red.** A period with no rows, a routine with no entry, a
  decision past its review date, a holdings snapshot older than the
  policy allows: findings of their own class, never "no data, no problem".
- **A4 Evidence cites.** Every finding names the policy id and the rows it
  rests on (`file:line`). A finding without rows is not a finding.
- **A5 Money is exact.** Amounts are Decimal in the row's currency. The
  base currency is declared once. A conversion uses the declared rates
  table for that date; without a rate the measure is `unknown`, and
  `unknown` is red.
- **A6 The ledger is ground truth.** Qayd changes nothing in the ledger. A
  correction is a new row that reverses an old one, so the history stays.
- **A7 Offline and silent.** No network, no bank, no broker, no price feed,
  no telemetry. Qayd reads files and writes reports.
- **A8 Never advises, never executes.** The output says what holds and what
  is breached. What to do about it is the owner's decision, recorded in
  decisions.yml with a review date (A3 then watches it).
- **A9 Public and synthetic.** Nothing from a real household enters this
  repository. The example data is generated by a committed script with a
  fixed seed.

## Roles
- **Builder** (Claude Code, cloud): executes HANDOFF tasks in order.
- **Reviewer** (read-only agent): compares the diff to HANDOFF and this
  constitution; raises findings; never edits.
- **Judge**: NEXT / REDO / STOP only. In doubt, STOP.
- **Commander** (the Table, acting for the owner): the only party that may
  widen scope, change the plan, or merge into branch `qayd`.
