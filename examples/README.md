# Example household

A synthetic household (constitution A9): every name and number here is
invented and generated by `tools/make_example.py` with seed 20261017.
Nothing comes from a real household. The script is the source of truth;
`tests/test_example.py` regenerates these files and compares them byte
for byte.

Files: `policies.yml` (8 policies, base OMR), `ledger.csv` (24 months,
2024-10 to 2026-09), `holdings.csv` (a snapshot on the last day of every
month), `decisions.yml` (4 decisions), `rates.csv` (USD on the first of
every month).

The story: a salary of 1200 OMR a month, rent, food, transport, family
support, a little discretionary spending, a 300 OMR transfer to savings
every month, and a small broker pilot (500 OMR into an equity ETF in
October 2024) that pays a quarterly dividend in USD.

## Planted facts

| when | what | policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-07 | discretionary spending 135+ OMR against 10% of 1200: `breach` | WLT-01 |
| 2025-11-30 | the ETF fell from 1450 to 1200 USD inside 90 days (17%): `kill` | WLT-05 |
| 2025-12-05 | decision D-2025-12-05 closes the kill; from that date the policy holds and says so | WLT-05 |
| 2026-03 | no transfer to savings: `missing` | WLT-03 |
| 2026-09-30 | decision D-2026-06-01 is open past its review date 2026-09-15: `DECISION-DUE` | - |

Everything else holds. So:

    python3 -m qayd check --dir examples/ --as-of 2026-09-30   # 8 hold, one DECISION-DUE, exit 1
    python3 -m qayd check --dir examples/ --as-of 2026-09-14   # all green, exit 0
    python3 -m qayd check --dir examples/ --as-of 2026-07-31   # WLT-01 breach
    python3 -m qayd check --dir examples/ --as-of 2026-03-31   # WLT-03 missing
    python3 -m qayd check --dir examples/ --as-of 2025-11-30   # WLT-05 kill
    python3 -m qayd check --dir examples/ --as-of 2025-12-31   # WLT-05 hold, closed by D-2025-12-05
    python3 -m qayd routine --dir examples/ --period 2026-03   # the missing transfer
    python3 -m qayd decisions --dir examples/ --as-of 2026-09-30
