# HANDOFF — M14.1b  (ADR-0007 Amendment BU, BU-1 to BU-5: the reader in a process of its own, counted by the watch)

Task: on Linux `raqib watch --host` starts a helper process that runs raqib/procevents.py's Reader, asks it once a round, and says in its report what was heard between two samples. Nothing is judged from it yet. Read ADR-0007's Amendments BQ, BT and BU first (grep for them; the file is long), then docs/specs/PROC-EVENTS.md, then raqib/own.py (how the watch records what it starts) and in raqib/host.py the live reader (`def live_reader` or the function whose docstring says "the reader owns an os_sysmon.Channel") and how a blind spot and a hole are said (`_blind`, `_hole`).

Scope:
  raqib/procevents.py (the serve mode and its client; the Reader's own behaviour is not changed), raqib/host.py (only: the Linux helper's start, ask, stop; `inv["procevents"]`; its holes and blind-spot words; the report's section), raqib/__main__.py or raqib/cli.py only if a module entry needs it (say which line), tests/test_procevents_helper.py (new), tests/test_host_procevents.py (new), tools/rig/procevents_run.py and tools/rig/check.py (only: X35 and the facts it needs), tests/test_procevents_rig.py and tests/test_rig_check.py (only X35), .github/workflows/rig.yml (only if X35 needs an argument), docs/specs/PROC-EVENTS.md, docs/specs/SERVICE.md (one paragraph: the unit's watch starts the helper), docs/GUIDE.md, docs/THREAT_MODEL.md, README.md (only where the sample report is the code's own and changes), docs/review/2026-11-20-M14.1b-questions.md; and a test of tests/ ONLY where it holds, word for word, a list, a sentence or a report this task changes (named in the questions file; no other assertion weakened).

Exit criteria:
- E1 (BU-1, the helper) `python -m raqib.procevents --serve` starts a Reader and then answers lines on its standard input: `read` -> one JSON line `{"processes": [...], "ends": [...], "older": [...], "more": bool, "counts": {...}}` holding at most 2000 entries in all (the rest with the next `read`, `more` true); `stop` or the end of its input -> it stops the Reader and exits 0. Its first line, before any ask, is `{"ready": null}` or `{"ready": "<the reader's refusal>"}` (then it exits 0). It reads nothing but its input, writes nothing but its output, starts no program. Tests drive it as a real child process on Linux (skipped elsewhere) and with fakes everywhere: the protocol, the bound and `more`, the end of input, a refusal, a line that is not a command (answered with `{"error": ...}`, the helper lives).
- E2 (BU-1, BU-2, the client) `procevents.Helper`: `start()` (the command is `[sys.executable]`, `-I` where the watch itself runs isolated, `-m raqib.procevents --serve`; started through the `started` callable it is given so the watch records it: raqib/own.py), `ask(timeout)` -> the answer or a reason (`late`, `ended`, `not a line`), `stop()` (closes the input, waits a bounded time, then ends it). It never blocks past its timeout (a thread or a selector reads the pipe); after a late answer the next `ask` does not take the late line for its own (answers carry the ask's number). Tests with a fake process and with the real helper on Linux.
- E3 (BU-2, BU-3, the watch) On Linux the live reader starts one Helper at its first round and asks it after each sample (timeout 2 s). `inv["procevents"]` = `{"began": n, "inside": n (began and ended since the last ask), "unread_exe": n, "lost": {...the reader's}, "refused": text or null, "hole": text or null, "more": bool}`. A refusal, a late answer, a helper that ended (started again once; a second end is final) are each a hole of a kind of its own, said once while it lasts. The helper is stopped when the watch stops (and by `close()` of a single look). On Windows and elsewhere no helper is started and the key is absent. A watch whose helper cannot start runs as before. Tests with a stand-in Helper: every case above; that no finding, no source line, no result and no exit code changes with the helper present (the same inventory with and without gives the same findings).
- E4 (BU-3, the report) A section `PROCESS EVENTS (Linux):` in the watch's report, after the CHANNEL line's place: rounds asked, processes that began, those that began and ended between two samples, programs not read, lost (the reader's words), and `not judged yet`. Where the reader was healthy for the whole watch the Linux words that nothing between two samples is seen say instead that it is heard and counted, not judged. Where it was not (refused, ended, lost) the old words stand and the reason is beside them. No program's name or path is printed (BU-4): a test feeds a path with control characters and asserts it is in no output, event or file.
- E5 (BU-5, the rig) tools/rig/check.py X35 (Linux; n/a elsewhere): the watch's report holds the section, its helper was not refused, nothing lost, and `began` is at least the number of children the stand-in said it started; MISSED where the section is absent or says a refusal on a kernel that answers (X31's rule for n/a). Tests feed made-up reports.
- E6 The documents: PROC-EVENTS.md (the serve protocol, the client, the bounds; BT-6's measurement as the reason), GUIDE.md (the report's section, what it means, that nothing is judged from it yet), THREAT_MODEL.md (what the helper adds and does not: a second process of Raqib's, ended with the watch; text a process chose reaches no output), SERVICE.md (one paragraph). A test holds the sentences that matter.
- E7 Gate green in the foreground; ASCII outside docs; no network; the helper and the client are the standard library's alone.
Not in scope: raqib/discover.py, any rule, a tree's source lines from the reader (M14.1c), connections (M14.2), Windows.
